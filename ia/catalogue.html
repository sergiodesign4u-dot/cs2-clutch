The daily free case is an addressable case, and the consequence is not free

An obligation written down before this node existed, and an orphan carried onto it rather than lost here.

What addressable means, concretely

It is a case. It has a tile, it sits in the grid, it carries the same four fields plus the daily marker 0.7 already holds, and it routes to 3.3 like any other case. The map's own words: I1 and I2 are components rather than screens, so the answer is not a surface, it is naming who owes it. Home owes the starter credit. This node owes the daily free case.

And the consequence, which is the part worth writing down: it can be filtered out of view

A banner survives every filter because it is not part of the list. A case does not. Filter to one category or to a price range and the daily free case leaves the grid with everything else that does not match. That is the price of the decision rather than a defect in it, and it is stated so that stage 04 does not quietly promote it back into a banner to keep it always visible.

Row I2 carries its orphan status onto this page

The daily free case is the only capability on the map with no parent in any of the three legal classes, placed in round 1 by founder decision of 11 August 2026. It runs against the residual risk named in the same document: a daily free open teaches, on a repeating schedule, the impression the rest of the map spends its budget contradicting, that opening is free. CLAUDE.md requires an orphan to be carried with its status printed in its own row. This is that row.

And on the same day, D-68: the daily case has no tile on this shelf at all. The Daily section is the ladder and nothing else, which is what the baseline does. That reverses this section and the argument below is kept as the cost: a banner survives every filter because it is not part of the list, and a case does not. The Daily section is now literally that panel outside the list, so no filter can empty it and it renders in every state of this page, including 3.2 when nothing matches at all. What answers the I2 risk is the ladder, not the tile, and that was true before the tile left. It closes a debt rather than opening one: where "once a day" and "taken today" render is answered, no field does, and 0.7's two daily states are void.

The inherited answer was handed on and it came back yes, D-67, 21 August 2026. The baseline gates its daily reward behind a wager requirement, 0.00 / 5.00, presented as a five tier ladder, and this node now carries it: component 0.15, in the Daily section, above that section's grid.

The section was refusing the wrong object. It exists to stop stage 04 promoting the daily case back into a banner, because a banner survives every filter and a case does not. That argument is untouched. The ladder is not the case: the ladder is the entitlement, the tile is the case, and the baseline has both in one Daily section. The tile is still addressable, still filterable, and on the filtered state it still leaves the grid.

The risk it was flagged for is unchanged and is not reduced by the answer. The ladder makes the daily case not free but earned, and the thing it is earned with is spending. Three rules travel with the component: never on a responsible play surface, never a suggestion of what to wager to a person with a limit in force, and a tier gives a case and nothing else.

Zero stock stays visible. Void since 21 August 2026, D-60, and kept for the argument

The one row in the bank that no competitor matches, and the reason is one sentence long.

Every item is current and available, so there is no shelf that can run short and nothing on this page reports one. D1 and A2 are void, node 3.4 is dissolved, and the free-unit count left the tile, the item card and the register. The guarantee moved one cluster away rather than disappearing: if a win cannot go to Steam there is a sale back to the site, D-38, or an exchange for a real skin, which is 5.3's to specify and does not exist yet. What follows is kept unedited as the record of a void rule.

An item at zero free units stays visible and marked rather than being filtered out, so the shelf is legibly empty instead of silently short. The flows state the reason in a line: hiding sold-out items would restore exactly the surprise D1 exists to remove.

On this page the figure is case level, and that distinction matters

The Case entity carries "free-unit availability across its table", A2, and the tile's stock marker is that figure, printed only when the shelf is short, D-24. A single item at zero free units is not a case level state, it is node 3.4 and it lives on the case screen, where the item card is. A case whose top item is out is still a case a person can open.

So there is no in-stock-only facet. At case level the predicate has no clean definition, and at item level the answer is already printed on the card.

The count obeys the register rather than a rule of its own. Every consumer of a published figure inherits all seven states of 0.11. The live free-unit count is a real-time row: a failing source renders as degraded and never as a frozen last good value, and missing renders as missing and never as a zero.

The risk band filter, refused here on 20 August and reversed on 21 August

The refusal read: nothing in this repository says how the band is computed, so 0.11 rule 1 applies, a figure ships with a route or it does not ship.

What overturned it is not an argument, it is the page. The live product computes the band, prints it on every tile as three marks in the band's colour, and filters by it with the same three named values.

What is still missing is smaller than the refusal said, and it is still missing. We do not have the thresholds. That moves from "no computation exists" to a question of fact to the live platform, in the same class as D-B. Until it is answered the band renders as not available on every tile and this facet cannot be applied, which is 0.11 rule 3 rather than a special case.

Sort, refused here on the bank and reversed on the product

The refusal read: no parent, the price facet already answers the budget question, and the bank has no T2 row for sorting. The bank is competitors. This is the product. There was no research against a sort control, only an absence in a competitor bank.

One limit travels with it and is not inherited. A sort by chance or by expected value would rank cases by how good a bet they look, on a page with no cost beside the ranking. 0.11 rule 7, a number is never a score. Sort by date and by entry cost. Never by chance, value, RTP or popularity.

C. SEO text, finished copy

What the numbers on a case mean. Every tile carries three things and each one is a fact you can check. The entry cost is what one open costs. The risk band says what the case is shaped like, High, Medium or Low, and it is read from the same drop table you can open and read yourself. Every item in that table shows its chance and what it is worth right now, and the chances add up to a hundred in front of you.

Where the chance and the value come from. The chance is the share of the roll interval the item occupies, and the interval is printed beside it, so the two are the same fact written twice. The value is a live market price with the moment it was read attached to it. Neither is a number we ask you to take on faith, and the case page shows the tested return and the expected value at that entry cost beside them.

The daily free case. One case a day opens without a balance. It sits in the grid with everything else rather than in a banner, so it is filtered and searched like any other case, and it shows the same chances and the same values as the ones that cost money.

This block stays the property of this node. Stage 05 aligns it to voice and syncs the final version back here. Interface strings do not move into it.

D. Structured data

CollectionPage plus ItemList. The members are the cases visibly in the grid, in the order they are visibly in. No Offer on this page: a list of links to cases is not a list of offers, and 0.13 places the offer on the case screen where the entry cost is the price of the thing bought.
