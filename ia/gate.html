The one thing this node is not

Identity verification. The split is D-17, and it is measured rather than argued.

Two layers, and the second one is somewhere else

Layer 1 was an 18+ self-declaration here. Layer 2 was identity verification before funding, at 2.7. The declaration is cheap on purpose and meets every visitor at the door, where conversion is most fragile. The expensive check meets the person at the money moment, where they are already committed and where the law actually attaches.

Both halves of that sentence have moved, and it is kept as the argument rather than as the rule. D-26 took the declaration off this node and put it in the consent gate of 2.4. The same decision took layer 2 out of round 1 and moved it from before funding to before withdrawal. So this node is not the cheap half of a two-layer split any more: it is the market question, and round 1 has exactly one age control and no identity control at all.

And the split is counted rather than asserted. The map puts the main job at three taps and this gate is one of them. The compliance layer eats a third of the budget for the main job. If identity verification sat at this tap the main job would be unreachable inside budget for a first-time visitor, which is why D-19 records the split and the tap count as one decision.

The honest limit, stated here rather than left for an audit to find

A self-declaration is not verification. aarrr.md already discarded soft age gating standing alone, and research.md section 7 records that checkbox age confirmation stopped being lawful for gambling sites in the UK from 25 July 2025. The UK is blocked at launch, so nothing is broken today.

What breaks is the day a market opens whose age method forbids a declaration, and that is exactly why age method is a field in 0.12 rather than a constant here. The word "only" in "the 18+ declaration only" is about scope, not about the number: Belgium is 21.

When it fires, and why never on arrival

Row B3 says "before any case interaction", not before any interaction, and the difference is the whole pre-login argument.

The category does the opposite, and it was opened live

The block bank records Hellcase serving a sign-in modal on arrival, before any content, carrying Steam, Google and Telegram, a terms agreement, and "I'm at the age of legal capacity (usually, 18 years) or older" as a checkbox inside it.

That is two barriers in one component: the trust evaluation cannot happen at all, and the age declaration is demoted to a line in a signup form. Verdict LEAVE, against B3 and D-17.

The four rules, and they are the register's rather than this node's

  1. A reason, always. "Not available in your country" with no ground reads as arbitrary because it is indistinguishable from arbitrary.
  2. Readable words, not a citation dump. The instrument lives in the register. The person gets one sentence they can act on.
  3. What is still open, stated in the same breath. Reading the site, the legal pages, support, and, where an account exists, the withdrawal.
  4. Never a soft 404 and never an error. 0.3 holds the error surfaces and this is not one of them.

200 with content, and it is an HTTP fact rather than a preference

Never 404, never 403, never a redirect to an error page. The reason is doubled: it is an answer rather than a missing page, and because the block renders at the URL the person is already on, there is nothing for a crawler to index and no market from which a crawler can be served the blocked page instead of the real one.

0.3 records the inverse failure live: Key-Drop returns HTTP 404 with the home page rendered in the body, so the person never learns the URL was wrong and the crawler drops the page. Both readers misled by one response. A geo block dressed as a 403 is the same defect pointed at a different audience.

A dead end for the right person is a success. For the wrong person it is a defect

Wrongly blocked gets the route to 0.10, which carries row G4: a stated ground and an appeal with a published response deadline. The deadline is [?] and it is 0.10's, not invented here.

Wrongly allowed is the expensive one and it is not this node's: later evidence at 4.1 or 2.7 moves the account to closing, where withdrawal stays open and the balance is frozen rather than zeroed.

What this state must never say

"Blocked" when the row is not launched, because that is a legal verdict we do not hold. A statute number as the whole message, because it is the ground's source and not the ground. "Temporarily unavailable" when nothing about it is temporary. An error code, a stack trace, or the word error. A list of the markets that are open. The footer's market statement is the public face of the register, 0.2, and it is crawlable everywhere. The footer carries no market statement since D-136, which removed its Where we operate row for having no destination. This dialog answers one person's question, it does not publish the register.

SEO and accessibility

No A to E block, and the reason is structural rather than an omission

This node has no URL, so it has no row in 0.13 section 3, no canonical, no schema and nothing to index. What it owes SEO is three properties, and all three are already fixed elsewhere:

  • It does not fire on arrival, so no public surface is gated for a person or a crawler, 0.13 section 4.3.
  • 2.2 returns 200 at the URL the person is already on, so the block cannot be indexed and cannot be served in place of a real page, 0.13 section 4.4.
  • One language, so no hreflang anywhere, 0.13 section 1.

Accessibility, and two of these are compliance rather than craft

  • The ground is text, never an image and never colour alone. A screen reader reads the reason and not only the refusal.
  • The declaration is a real control with an accessible name that states the age, not a styled div and not a label the control does not carry. The declaration left for 2.4 with D-26, and the rule travels with it.
  • Focus moves into the dialog on open and is trapped, and returns to the control that triggered it on dismissal.
  • Escape closes it, and closing records nothing.
  • The route out of 2.2 is a crawlable <a href>, 0.13 section 8. A destination reachable only by script does not exist for a keyboard either.
