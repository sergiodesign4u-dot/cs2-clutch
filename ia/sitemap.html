Two things this diagram makes visible that the intent list could not

Cluster 3 is the hub of the product, with seven edges. Everything either leads into choosing and opening or comes out of it. That is the main job holding its place structurally rather than only in the priority list.

Cluster 6 is entered from two places and exits to none. A boundary screen that routed onward would be a boundary that suggests a next move. It is the only cluster in the map with no outgoing edge, and that is the shape the compliance rule in CLAUDE.md asks for.

Amended on 18 August 2026 by D-26

Cluster 2 was 9 nodes and 9 MVP. It is now 8 and 5: 2.3 dissolved because the age control became a checkbox rather than a screen, and 2.7, 2.8 and 2.9 went LATER with identity verification.

Sixteen LATER nodes now, not thirteen, and the three new ones sit in cluster 2 rather than moving to cluster 8, because they are parked rather than reclassified. They are carried on the map rather than deleted so the free-entry withdrawal hole stays visible.

Fifty-three MVP nodes against twelve MVP screens, and the ratio is the point

Each screen carries roughly three states or dialogs that need their own specification, plus fourteen shell nodes nobody counted as screens at all. Twenty six of the fifty-three are file-level, which is what step 6 actually builds, one md plus one html each. The remaining 27 are states specified inside their parent under their own anchor.

The count has moved twice, and both times from work rather than from a recount. On 11 August node 0.2 promised six destinations and the map held four, so 0.9 and 0.10 were registered before that specification was written. This is the growth the pack calls discover-as-you-go, and it is the reason the rule is worded as a prohibition: a carrier may not promise a destination the map does not hold.

On 12 August the global sweep added 0.11 to 0.14, and not one of them is a new surface. Round 1 is still the nine surfaces D-20 locked. Three are structures those nine already read, described once instead of nine times, and the fourth is the component that renders the product's evidence. The verdict on every candidate, kept and rejected, with the consumer count that decided it, is on Structure.

A dialog is file-level only when it is a destination. The age and geo gate and the cookie banner are: they carry their own content, they fire across the whole product, and they have routes out. The self exclusion confirmation is not: it guards one action on one screen. Without that line the word dialog would have produced two different file counts depending on who read it.

The base layer's LATER count was wrong, and it is corrected here

Four files carried "12 MVP screens, 12 LATER screens, 24 in the map": CLAUDE.md, README.md, ia/docs/sitemap.md and the concept map. The LATER screens are thirteen, and the map holds twenty-five. Base-layer step 7 restored Top wins as the seventh orphan and updated the orphan count in the prose without updating the budget line that prose fed. Both readings then sat in the same file.

Recorded as a class rather than as a typo: a count in prose and a count in a budget line are two sources for one fact, and they drifted within one step of each other. Corrected in all four places by this step.

What this page does not draw

Stated so that nobody reads more into the map than it holds.

No page contents. Blocks, states in full, components and SEO belong to the node specification, one per file-level node, reached from Structure. The INCLUDES line here is a summary, not a specification.

No block composition, and deliberately not yet. What a page is made of is the most expensive structural decision in the product, and it is taken at step 4 from a bank built out of competitors opened in a live browser plus out-of-category craft references, never from a model's median. Any block list written before that bank exists would be exactly that median.

No routes at node level. Decisions, dead ends and recoveries are drawn in Flows and stay the source for them. The cluster map above is a summary of those flows, not a replacement.

No colour anywhere in the product mockups. Node visuals are black and white at wireframe level through this whole stage. Colour, brand and imagery arrive at stage 06. The colour on this documentation page is the site theme and a different thing entirely.
