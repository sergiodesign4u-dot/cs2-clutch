Two things this diagram makes visible that the intent list could not

Cluster 3 is the hub of the product, with seven edges. Everything either leads into choosing and opening or comes out of it. That is the main job holding its place structurally rather than only in the priority list.

Cluster 6 is entered from two places and exits to none. A boundary screen that routed onward would be a boundary that suggests a next move. It is the only cluster in the map with no outgoing edge, and that is the shape the compliance rule in CLAUDE.md asks for.

Fifty-three MVP nodes against twelve MVP screens, and the ratio is the point

Each screen carries roughly three states or dialogs that need their own specification, plus fourteen shell nodes nobody counted as screens at all. Twenty six of the fifty-three are file-level, thirty are, recounted 23 August 2026 against ia/_nav.js, which is what step 6 actually builds, one md plus one html each. The old figure was three behind before D-90: D-36 added 5.9, 5.10 and 5.11 on 20 August and this sentence was not moved. The rest are states specified inside their parent under their own anchor.

The count has moved twice, and both times from work rather than from a recount. On 11 August node 0.2 promised six destinations and the map held four, so 0.9 and 0.10 were registered before that specification was written. This is the growth the pack calls discover-as-you-go, and it is the reason the rule is worded as a prohibition: a carrier may not promise a destination the map does not hold.

On 12 August the global sweep added 0.11 to 0.14, and not one of them is a new surface. Round 1 is still the nine surfaces D-20 locked. Three are structures those nine already read, described once instead of nine times, and the fourth is the component that renders the product's evidence. The verdict on every candidate, kept and rejected, with the consumer count that decided it, is on Structure.

A dialog is file-level only when it is a destination. The age and geo gate and the cookie banner are: they carry their own content, they fire across the whole product, and they have routes out. The self exclusion confirmation is not: it guards one action on one screen. Without that line the word dialog would have produced two different file counts depending on who read it.
