Why this is a node

Registered by the global sweep at step 3 on 12 August 2026, after it counted five consumers.

The backlog argues for it, and the footer proved it within a week

Rows A4 and G3 are described in cjm-to-be.md as "the same feature, two surfaces". Node 0.2 made it a third one step after that sentence was written, and did so while asserting a reconciliation route that does not exist.

That is the failure mode a register prevents. A number defined at each surface is a number that will eventually disagree with itself, and on this product the disagreement lands on design principle 1.

What a register is, and what stage 04 does with it

A node read by several nodes and drawn by none. Not a page, because nobody navigates to it. Not a component, because nothing renders it. Stage 04 does not wireframe this node. What it inherits from here is the state set and the anatomy below, and each consuming node carries its own figures into its own wireframe.

Read by 1.0 Home, 1.2 Provably fair, 3.3 Case screen, 5.3 Withdrawal and 0.2 Footer. Rendered by 0.6 and 0.7 where a number sits on a card.

The condition standing above every row

D-C, from the open decisions in cjm-to-be.md. It is not a design question and it outranks all of them.

The observed-against-published check runs first, and once published it never comes down

Every capability in this register is a commitment device that only works for an operator whose published weights are already true. The check has to run internally on the live platform's data first, and if it does not hold, that is a business decision preceding the design one. cjm-to-be.md also records the other half: once the check ships to the public, it is never taken down.

Which makes this the node where the cost of principle 1 becomes concrete. Publishing a number here is not a display decision, it is a permanent commitment. The work below assumes the answer is yes. If it is not, the rows conditional on it are not redesigned, they are withdrawn, which is why the state matrix carries a withdrawn state.

D-C is scheduled before stage 04. Nothing in this register is drawn until it returns.

What A1 requires on this product now, and whether it is met

This register's answer to the second half of D-92, and it is not a tick. Naming the venue did not close A1. It changed what closing it means.

What the outbound link was actually buying, and it was two jobs on one control

A1 verbatim: "Two prices, one item: live market price with timestamp beside the exact chance at this entry cost, an outbound market link on the top item, and the cheaper-on-average statement in our own voice." Parents B1-2, pattern of 14, the strongest pattern in the corpus, and B2-1.

B1-2 is people teaching each other to buy the skin outright instead of opening for it. The concession has two halves and A1 hangs both on a single link. Verification: a person leaves, reads the price somewhere we do not control, comes back, and the proof is that we did not host the number. Concession: here is the cheaper way to get this item, shown at the cost of our own sale.

On this product those two point at different places, and that is the whole of the difficulty. Steam is checkable and dearer: the two walked captures put a copy from us 39 percent and 27 percent under the Steam listing. Our own listing is cheaper and verifies nothing. A1 assumed one object where this product has two.

Why the trade is still the right one, and this is not a consolation prize

Against an outside venue our own two numbers were opaque and the venue was checkable, so a sceptic could verify everything except the thing they actually doubted. Against Steam our numbers become checkable as a stated relation, which is design principle 1 in the exact D-14 wording, "the product is built so that its own numbers are checkable". What a B1-2 sceptic disputes is our pricing, not Steam's. The relation answers that and the outbound link never did.

And what the trade does not cover: the label. Shipping "buy it on the market instead" unchanged turns an honesty gesture into advice that costs the reader money while we sell the same copy cheaper. That is not a string for stage 05 to tidy: it is a requirement that has to be re-decided before there is a string to write.

Ten rules every published number obeys

Each one derived from a row, a decision or a principle, never from taste.

  1. A route, or it does not ship. Every figure points at something a stranger can open, compute or wait for. D-14: a number without one is decoration that looks like evidence.
  2. An as-of moment on anything that moves. Row A1 requires the timestamp by name, and the rule generalises: a figure with no time attached is read as current forever.
  3. Missing is a state, never a zero. A dash that reads as zero and a zero meaning "we do not know" are the same lie in two typefaces. CLAUDE.md: unknown is [?].
  4. Below the sample floor, print N rather than a percentage. D-B says the observed rate counter is worth nothing at N = 0. A percentage over a handful of rolls is not a smaller truth, it is a different claim.
  5. A configuration change forks the counter. From row D3 verbatim: the old configuration keeps its history and stays visible, the new one starts at zero and says so. A counter that silently absorbs a weights change is worse than no counter.
  6. A failed source is marked degraded, never frozen at its last good value. The Steam health probe in G2 is the pattern, and it applies to every real-time figure here.
  7. A number is never a score. No summing across kinds, which is why the header's two money figures are never added, node 0.1. No streaks, no progress, no celebration, the rule C5 attaches to limits: the moment a figure acquires completion mechanics it stops describing and starts encouraging. One named exception, and it is named rather than quietly allowed: the favourite count on node 0.7, founder decision D-24 of 18 August 2026. It is a count of accounts that marked a case, not a rank and not a rating. Still refused: a rank, a default sort, a most-popular strip, a badge on a top tile, or the count beside an open control on 3.3. One count in one place, and not a precedent for a second.
  8. Text, never an image. A figure baked into a picture is invisible to search, to a screen reader, and to a person copying it into a complaint.
  9. A number about someone else carries its source and its date, or it is [?]. The rule this project applies to its own research, applied to product copy, because a comparative claim in the interface is a claim about a third party.
  10. A money figure carries its unit, and a coin figure carries its peg wherever money is spent. D-28 of 19 August 2026 reversed C1 and made the coin the product's denomination, which removed the structural answer to B7-1, the currency abstraction that hides what things cost. What is left of that answer is this rule, and the decision ships on it rather than beside it. Wherever a person is about to spend, commit or withdraw, the coin figure renders with what one coin is worth in real money, from a published rate carrying its own as-of under rule 2. Three consequences, and each is a place this rule is checked. A coin figure with no peg within reach of the spend is the abstraction B7-1 names, not a shorter label. Time and ratios do not become coins: A4 and G3 stay minutes, D4 stays a percentage, and converting either would be summing across kinds under rule 7. A figure quoted from outside stays in its own unit: A1's Steam market price is real money, and until the peg is published it is marked as not comparable rather than set silently beside a coin value.

What folds at 360 and what does not

The comparison folds first, then the caption shortens. The as-of and the route never fold, because those two are exactly what turns a number into evidence. A figure stripped to value and caption on mobile is the category's own habit and it is the one thing this register forbids.

SEO and accessibility

The register has no public URL. It constrains how figures behave on the indexed pages that read it.

Every figure is crawlable text, rule 8, which is also the "text not in images" line in the A to E checklist. The pre-login figures on 1.0 and 3.3 are the product's entire pre-login argument, and an image would remove them from search and from a screen reader in one stroke.

The accessible name carries the unit and the as-of, not the bare digits. A screen reader announcing "82.1" has announced nothing.

Real-time counters do not shout. A live region firing on every round is a denial of service to a screen reader. Real-time figures update silently by default with the current value always available on demand, and where an update genuinely changes what a person can do the announcement is polite and rate limited. A design rule with a stated reason, not a quoted success criterion.

A degraded or missing figure is announced as such, in text, in the block. Colour alone never carries that state, which also survives stage 06 turning the colour on.

Numbers carry no schema of their own. Where a page type takes structured data, node 0.13 assigns it.
