Identity verification

Out of round 1 by D-26, 18 August 2026, and kept on the map rather than deleted

The founder's ground is a walked fact: the account capture records the entire settings surface of the live product and there is no identity step anywhere in it. The specification below is not withdrawn. It is parked, and four costs travel with it in the open.

1. The free-entry withdrawal hole stays open. A person who only ever took free entries reaches a real withdrawal with no identity check. This node was the answer; without it the hole is permanent rather than pending. 2. The licence direction loses its enforcement point. D-23 chose the Isle of Man, and a tier one regulator requires identity before withdrawal. 3. The legal exposure of each method, from the To-Be map rather than from anyone's judgement

Facial age estimation is fast and recognised as effective in strict regimes, and it is contested biometric data under GDPR and under state statutes such as Illinois BIPA.

Full document KYC before deposit removes the failure entirely, pays the largest conversion cost, creates a document trove, and may itself be read as the operator self-classifying as a gambling business in a category where that classification is unsettled.

Payment-instrument attestation is nearly invisible, its coverage varies sharply by country, and it is ruled out standing alone on a cited statute, the UK Online Safety Act of July 2025.

Nothing above is chosen here. Picking a biometric vendor from an unresearched comparison is the median-instead-of-a-source failure this project's rules exist to prevent.

The hole this node inherits and does not close

Both the flows and the map record it: a person who only ever uses free entry, the starter credit I1 or the daily free case I2, can reach withdrawal and take out a real skin without ever meeting an identity check, because B1 gates funding and B2 forbids the check at the exit, and somebody who never funds never meets the gate.

The shape that closes it without reopening B8-4 is already proposed and deliberately not drawn: raise the check when the account first holds a withdrawable item, at the outcome, so the person learns it with the item in hand and nothing lost, rather than at the exit with everything staked.

It stays undrawn because it is a compliance decision riding on D-A, which counsel owns, and drawing an unconfirmed legal route into the information architecture would be the median this project's rules exist to prevent. Carried unchanged. Not solved, not softened, not quietly added as a block.

Block 8 is the one most likely to be cut for tidiness

B2 is a code rule with no interface. A person standing in front of a check they may fail cannot read our code. Block 8 is where that rule becomes something they can see.

No block for: a balance figure, a case image, a ticker, a bonus, a "you are almost there" celebration, or a progress bar that congratulates. The step indicator counts steps. It never scores. 0.11 rule 7: a number is never a score, no streaks, no progress, no celebration.

When the document says a different market

The identity document is the strongest evidence the product ever sees, and the register's precedence rule is that later evidence overrides earlier.

The case is designed rather than met: a person passes the IP gate at 2.1, arrives here, and the document says a market the register does not open.

That is not a failed check and it must not render as one. The procedure belongs to 0.12 and every step of it is owned elsewhere: deposits and opening stop; withdrawal stays open; the balance is frozen, never zeroed; written notice with the ground in words; an appeal with a published response deadline; the row moves to closing, then to blocked once the exit window ends, and the window length is [?].

The outcome is closing, not 2.9. The person's identity was verified. It is the market that is refused, and telling them their identity failed would be false. Two different refusals with two different grounds, and merging them is the same defect as merging blocked with not launched at 2.2.

Two states added to the map, named rather than smuggled in

Session expired or left mid-flow. Its parent is design principle 5 in CLAUDE.md, quoted: "Speed is trust. Fast load, fast reveal, fast withdrawal status." Re-doing a completed step is time taken from a person for our convenience, on the screen where they are least willing to spend it. Printed with its parent rather than assumed, so that if the parent reads thin the state is cut rather than defended on taste.

Market refused on the document. Its parent is row B4 and the register's precedence rule. Added here because the register named 2.7 as a consumer and no node had yet said what the consumer does with the answer.

What it must never do

  • Ban, wipe or go silent. Barrier B8-3, pattern of 3, and its injury is the silence rather than the restriction: "they refused to provide any specific explanation".
  • Refuse without a ground. A ground the person cannot read is the same as no ground.
  • Present a verdict as final when an appeal exists, or present an appeal as a formality when it is not.
  • Route to 5.3 through another check. Row B2. The exit link goes to the exit.

Retry is open and is not decided here

Whether a second attempt at the check is allowed at all depends on the method and on what counsel permits, so it is [?]. What is fixed: the appeal is the default route, and if a retry exists it is bounded rather than a loop.

And the shape is not 2.3's: an under-age declaration is a statement the person made, while a failed check is a claim about our evidence, and a claim about our evidence is exactly the kind of thing an appeal is for.
