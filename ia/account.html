The sentence this page is built around

One line of the backlog, and it decides the whole composition.

Row F2, with its own success signal

"The receipt stays on the item in inventory and on the withdrawal record." Signal: the same receipt is retrievable a week later.

Barrier B7-1 is not a complaint about losing. It is a complaint about a number that stopped being true and left nothing behind to check: "a knife that Hellcase displayed as being worth about $1,600. After withdrawing it, I found out that on CSFloat and even on Steam it is worth only around $550-600."

The injury is retrospective. A page that shows today's price and nothing else cannot be wrong a week later, because there is nothing left to compare it against.

So the page holds two dated figures per item and never merges them

The receipt as captured at the open, with its own as-of. The current value, with its own as-of. Overwrite the first and the person cannot check what we told them. Show only the first and we are printing a stale price as current, which is the barrier itself.

And no delta

No arrow, no percentage, no up-or-down colour, no badge. Two dated figures let a reader do the subtraction. A rendered delta turns a held skin into a position and the page into a portfolio. In-platform exchange is LATER by scope, 0.11 rule 7 forbids a figure that becomes a score, and a gain indicator on an inventory is a reason to keep going, which is the defect the limits rule names for boundaries.

Two fields of 0.6 are deliberately not rendered here: drop chance and ticket range. Both are properties of a case, not of a held instance. The two venues on the receipt are [?] and that stays with 0.11, which owns row F1.

The thirty day question

The block bank opened Hellcase's public FAQ on 12 August 2026 and found a real product rule living in a document.

The rule, verbatim, from a page opened live

"Your drop is waiting for up to 30 days. Full cost of the item will be returned to your balance if you don't withdraw it within 30 days."

A deadline that converts an item into balance is the difference between holding a skin and holding a countdown, and the person meets it by having already missed it.

Whether we carry any such deadline is [?] and it is a product decision, not a design one. What is decided here is the slot and its behaviour, because row G5 requires a limit to be stated before it runs rather than discovered afterwards.

The facet question the sweep left open, answered

The global sweep left exactly one instruction for this step, and it is answered rather than carried further.

The instruction: "Facet and filter system, 3.1 only. Not global. Revisit at step 6 if 5.1 acquires filters of its own: two listings sharing a facet set makes it canonical, one listing does not."

Answered: 5.1 does not take the 3.1 facet set, so the facet system stays local to the catalogue

An inventory is bounded by what one person opened. The catalogue's filters exist because a shelf of hundreds cannot be read. A holding of a handful can.

A filter on an inventory manufactures a second empty state with no parent. An empty filtered inventory would need removable chips to escape a problem the page does not have, and 5.2 is an empty with a completely different meaning: nothing held, route to the catalogue.

What is proposed instead, named out loud rather than added quietly

A sort by value or by date acquired, parent Related Job 5, because a person opening this page to withdraw is looking for one item and value is how they name it. It is conditional and its trigger is [?]: it ships when a typical holding does not fit one screen, and typical holding size is not a number anyone has. Until then the list is ordered most recent first, which is the order the person acquired them in and the only order this node can justify.

The refusal is the design

The obvious move on an empty inventory is a re-entry offer, and the daily free case I2 and the starter credit I1 are both sitting in the backlog waiting to be placed. They are not placed here.

The person arriving after a low-value first open is at the emotional floor of the whole map. Barrier B7-3 records the one voice in the corpus that criticises the consolation frame itself: "it just gives incentive to people in spending more and fools them into thinking that the end result isn't that bad". An empty inventory answered with an offer to open again is that pattern with a different label.

Free entry already has its homes, on 1.0 as an offer and in 3.1 as an available case. A third one invented here would be a page specification placing a capability the map already placed. And I2 is the only row on the whole backlog with no parent in the three legal classes, which makes it the last capability that should be given a new site by a node.

The route to the catalogue is the offer.
