The one thing that separates this from a help page

Barrier B8-3 is three reviews, and the second one names the defect exactly.

What the evidence actually complains about

"My account was suddenly banned, excluded from the Rain system, and my balance of over 160 coins was completely wiped out... they refused to provide any specific explanation."

"as soon as I tried to withdraw my winnings, the site permanently banned me. They took my deposited skins and locked my account."

"banning a user just to avoid paying out a small $80 prize."

Not one of those three is a complaint about slow support. They are complaints about a decision with no ground and no route.

So the answer is four things, not a help centre with a form at the bottom

  1. A written ground, produced by the surface that took the decision, G4
  2. A route to dispute it, public, on this page
  3. A published deadline for an answer, and 0.11 already holds the row
  4. A state for the deadline being missed, because a success signal with no failure state is a metric nobody has to meet

blocks.md section 8 takes Hellcase's public FAQ structure and refuses its placement. Their FAQ holds real product rules, the thirty day holding deadline and the crediting window, which a person meets in the product and reads about afterwards. The FAQ repeats a rule. It never owns one.

The service level, which is one number and four rules

0.11 section 2 already holds the row: published appeal response deadline, parent G4 on B8-3, read on 5.3, 2.9 and 0.10, static until republished. Its value is [?] and this node does not invent one.

1. One number, one unit, everywhere it is rendered

5.6, 1.4, 2.2 and this page all print the same figure from the same row, and 2.9 will when it returns. A deadline restated per surface is a deadline that will disagree with itself, which is the whole argument for 0.11 existing.

2. It counts from ticket creation, not from the first time a human reads it

A clock that starts when we pick the ticket up is a clock we control.

3. It is a deadline for an answer, not for a resolution, and the difference is printed

An answer is substantive: what we decided, on what ground, and what happens next. "We are looking into it" does not stop the clock, because an autoresponder that satisfies the deadline reproduces the exact sentence B8-3 records: they refused to provide any specific explanation.

4. If no deadline is published, the page says so

0.11 rule 3: missing is a state, never a zero, and never a plausible placeholder. Nobody will ship that sentence, which is precisely why the rule is worth writing.

And one number proposed rather than asserted

Our own median time to answer, published from our own logs, which is the A4 and G3 treatment applied to support. It is not a backlog row today, and it is recorded in the treatment 0.11 section 4 gives every proposal, so nobody later ships it as though it had been approved.

1. The FAQ holds no number of its own

Every figure in an answer is read from 0.11, or the answer links to the surface and prints none. Hellcase's FAQ carries the thirty day holding deadline and the crediting window as facts, and blocks.md section 5 records both as real product rules living in a document.

2. No answer is the only place a rule appears

If it is, the rule is missing from the product and that FAQ entry is a bug report. This is a mechanical acceptance criterion for stage 04, not a sentiment.

3. The FAQ is below the substance, never instead of it

Refero Ableton, TAKE. The contact route, the appeal route and the deadline come first. The FAQ is not the page, and sitemap.md records why: the baseline's FAQ was the whole of its age control and the whole of its geo statement, both jobs moved to 2.1 and 6.1, and what arrived here is the residue.

4. Collapsed for reading, never for existence

Every accordion is a <button> with aria-expanded and aria-controls, and every answer is in the DOM at every width. Same rule as 0.2 and 0.1.

Which questions exist is not written here. They are derived at stage 05 from the As-Is barrier ledger, one question per documented barrier that survives its surface. This node fixes the sections and the four rules.

What happens now

Why that state exists at all

G4's success signal is that response time stays inside the published deadline. A signal with no state for its own failure is a number nobody has to meet, and the failure would then be met as an incident with no interface. It is the same argument 0.14 makes about its mismatch state, in a different place, and it ends in the same sentence from the evidence: they refused to provide any specific explanation.

Five rules that are layout rather than styling

The prefilled ground wraps and never truncates, and never sits in a fixed-height scroller. A ground a person has to scroll inside a box is a ground they will not read, and G4 exists so it gets read.

The ticket id follows 0.14 section 5: monospace, always text and never an image, copy as a first-class control rather than a hover affordance, truncated in the middle if it must truncate at all. It exists to be pasted somewhere else, exactly like a hash.

The deadline carries its unit in the accessible name, not only in the visual, per 0.11 section 8.

The form's errors name the field and the fix, which is B5's rule for readable failure states applied to our own form. A support form that fails unreadably is the barrier arriving on the page built to answer it.

The two entry points are two headings and two forms, not tabs. A tab hides one of two routes from a person who arrived needing the other. Every target at least 44 by 44 CSS pixels, matching 0.1.

Desktop, from 900px. The two routes sit side by side, the FAQ stays one column with its section list beside it per the Refero WhatsApp row in the bank, and the deadline stays with the H1 and never moves into a margin.

E. Optimisation checklist

  1. Exactly one H1, and it is the page's job in words
  2. The H2s exist in block order and match list B
  3. A visible breadcrumb is drawn and its markup matches it
  4. The email address is a crawlable mailto: link and is also selectable text
  5. Canonical self, no hreflang
  6. No text in images, the deadline above all: it is the page's one published number
  7. Every FAQ answer is in the DOM at every width, collapsed rather than absent
  8. The page renders without script. A support route behind a client-side renderer is unreachable exactly when the product is failing
