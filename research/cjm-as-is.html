01 - Focus

Persona x job, and the rule the whole map obeys

One persona, one job, one half of the map. The As-Is half is a mirror of the present, so it is held to a stricter evidence rule than anything else in this stage.

Primary persona
The Opener
Why primary, verbatim from personas.md:95: "Every competitor design decision confirms this archetype. The reveal animation is the product. This person drives the most volume and is the first user any platform must succeed with."
The main job, taken from jtbd.md:17
When I am embedded in the CS2 ecosystem and want excitement beyond the game itself, I want a chance at a rare skin I actually want, with a reveal that feels real and unpredictable, so that I get the rush of a genuine drop and potentially end up with something worth having.

Three non-identical wordings of this job exist across jtbd.md:17, personas.md:107 and CLAUDE.md:46. The map uses the JTBD file, because the JTBD file owns job formulas. The divergence is carried open, not resolved here.

The asymmetry of honesty, the rule of this half

Every emotion and every barrier stands on a source. No source means [?], not a drawn emotion. A phase with evidence for both poles and no way to weigh them gets no value at all rather than an invented net.

Every count on this page is checkable. The quotes behind it live in research/docs/cjm-evidence.md, one row each with platform, rating, date and URL. No number here exceeds what that ledger holds. Where the mining pass counted more than the ledger can show, the number was reduced to the ledger.

Signal against pattern, the definition the chart depends on
Signal

One mention, or several mentions inside a single thread, because the people in one thread are answering each other.

Pattern

The same thing said by people who are not in the same conversation. The count is always given and always matches the ledger.

A signal never outranks a pattern, and an MVP feature is not built on a signal without saying so out loud.

Is this the only map needed

For round 1, yes. The Battler's path diverges fundamentally (lobby, human opponent, simultaneous reveal) but battle mode is hidden at launch (jtbd.md:201), so it earns its own map in a later round. The Researcher diverges by channel rather than by structure, so it is marked with one line rather than drawn as a second map. The Accumulator's path is a post-D1 loop and sits outside this job.

Three limits that shape everything below, stated before the data rather than after it

1. Reddit was unreachable

Every route was tried: old.reddit.com, www.reddit.com, site-restricted search, the PullPush and Arctic Shift archive APIs, five Redlib and Teddit mirrors, and the Wayback Machine. All blocked at the fetch layer. Steam Community and HLTV substitute for it. A named hole, not a silent substitution.

2. A Trustpilot brand page is not a sample

key-drop shows an aggregate of 4.3 while its first page is almost entirely one star. skin.club shows 3.6 while its first page is entirely five stars. No proportion is claimed anywhere, only counts of distinct people, and only across sources that are not the same conversation.

3. The boards select for the moment things go wrong

People post when they lost, got scammed, or are trying to quit. The satisfied regular is largely absent by construction. The negativity below is real as evidence of what the bad path feels like, and it is not a base rate.

Sources actually opened

Trustpilot brand pages for hellcase, key-drop, csgoroll, skin.club, daddyskins, datdrop, clash.gg, captured in a live browser with proof screenshots in research/screens/cjm/. Sitejabber, now hosted at smartcustomer.com. Steam Community discussions. HLTV.org forums. Google Play listings through the AppBrain mirror. YouTube comment threads, opened in a live browser at step 6. Operator help centres, which count as policy statements, not as user voice.

02 - As-Is path

Eight phases, P1 to P8

The phases are derived from the real sequence of this job as recorded in ux-patterns.md:21-27, the only enumerated step sequence for this persona in the corpus, not from a generic awareness-to-retention funnel. Three phases carry no emotional value after mining and after re-research, and they are drawn as empty rather than filled.

What the skeleton predicted against what mining found The step 3 skeleton named P3 and P4 as the two phases with no documented content. Mining filled P4 with three barriers and a value of -4. P3 stayed undetermined, and P5 and P6 joined it. The three empty phases on this page are P3, P5 and P6.

P7
The outcome
-5 patterns of 7 and 11

Goal

Understand what they got, feel something about it, and know what to do next.

Actions

Reacts. Shares, if sharing is possible. Opens again, converts the item, or stops. No competitor has confirmed one-tap sharing from the result state.

Touchpoints

Result screen, inventory, share affordance where one exists.

Thoughts

The central finding of the mining pass: the loss is almost never described as losing. People describe one of two other things, a win that turned out to be worth less than it looked, and a published probability that did not behave. "I am in the cycle of go lose go lose go lose" (Steam, Crash).

What the two forms mean together

The user does not demand a win. They demand that the number the platform published behaves like a number, and that the value the platform displayed survives contact with the market. Both are verifiability complaints wearing the clothes of a bad-luck complaint.

03 - Emotional curve

The curve, and its three breaks

Scale runs from -5 to +5. Every point is one emotion carried up from its phase with its source already attached there, and every count matches cjm-evidence.md. Nothing is interpolated: three phases have no honest value and are plotted as breaks in the line, not smoothed through.

The chart scrolls sideways inside its own box on a narrow screen.

Pattern point

A full-size point on a solid line. The emotion is stated by people who are not in the same conversation, and the count is given and matches the ledger.

Signal point

A small point on a thinner line. One mention, or several inside a single thread. A signal never outranks a pattern.

[?] gap

A hollow grey marker resting on the zero line, which is not a value. The line breaks here and is never drawn through. Three of eight phases are in this state.

Counter-current

A simultaneous opposite emotion inside the same phase, plotted as a second thin line rather than averaged into the main one. Recorded at P1 and at P6, and drawn only there.

Two things the drawing is saying out loud First: every plotted point in this map rests on a pattern. The signal style is defined in the legend because the phase table uses signal strength for the three [?] phases, where it never becomes a value. Second: the counter-current is recorded at two phases only, so it is drawn at two phases only. Joining P1 to P6 across four phases that hold no counter-current value would be interpolation, which this map forbids.

The largest structural change

Three of eight phases now carry no value: P3, P5 and P6. Two were already undetermined in substance and one was carrying a number it could not support. The curve is a broken line in three places, and each break is a statement rather than a gap in the work.

The largest change of meaning

An earlier draft concluded that the curve spikes to its maximum at the reveal and falls immediately after. That reading is gone. What the evidence supports is starker: the only phase with an evidenced positive emotion is P1, before the user has spent anything, and it is an emotion about someone else's outcome.

Video comment threads, opened in a live browser, gave P1 its first direct evidence: six spectator reactions on a third-party site's thread and two first-person statements of the "why not me" mechanism. They also produced the only user observation about the visual treatment of a reveal anywhere in this research, and confirmed the hole at P6 rather than filling it.
