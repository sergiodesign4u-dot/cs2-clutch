Support

The panel holds components and never redraws them: the Field in its row form (the four limits, level 2), the Text field (the amount), the Popover menu in its select form (the period), the Button (each act, the quiet ghost at M: the page refuses a lead to one control, microcopy 6.1-61), the Key-value line (.kv--rule, which way a limit moves), the Note line (what a limit closes, the answer to a press) and the Text link (help that is not ours), all level 1 but the Field. The Section head and the Prose beside it are the screen's. The panel's own part is the status line, the in-force plate and its list, the two cards of what a limit closes, the list of rows and each row's control line, the hidden name of a period, the place of the answer and the help line; it sizes the amount, the period and the acts into columns so the four rows line up.

Variants and sizes

One axis of data and one of width. Sizes are read from the render.

  • State = what is set. None set: the status line in the body ink and no in-force plate. In force (a cool down, a deposit limit): the line said in white and the plate first. Excluded: the plate names the exclusion and its end, and what still works. The rows stay the same four in every state; a press answers under its row.
  • Width = the measure, then the row. The panel never grows past 720px. From 600px the amount, the period and the act stand on one line at the row's right edge, in columns the four rows share; under 600px the amount takes its own line under the words, and the period and the act share the next one where both fit.

When to use

Only on Responsible play. A limit is a boundary a person sets for themselves: the panel states what is in force and when it ends as a moment, and says what a limit never closes (taking what you hold out to Steam, support, reading the product). It never acquires completion mechanics, streaks, status or a session score (CLAUDE.md, "Product substance"): at that point it would stop being a boundary and become a reason to keep going.

Rule and anti-rule

Use it

Responsible play: the four limits as rows, each act a quiet ghost, the answer under the row that was pressed, the confirmation of a self exclusion in a Dialog.

Take another component

A limit that stops a deposit is said on Deposit by the Status notice, not by this panel. A setting with no period and no amount is a Field row on its own. A figure that counts down or fills a bar is never drawn for a limit, by any component.

States

No states under the pointer: the panel is not interactive. What it holds keeps its own (the amount's focus and refusal ring are the Text field's, the period opens as the Popover menu, the acts press as the Button). Two things are set by the product's script, rp.js, which joins the system at step 8: the answer to a press, written under its row as a Note line, saved in white (.note--hi, rename map row 25, where kit.css drew .limits__answer:not(.note--refuse)) or refused (.note--refuse); and the status line, said (.is-said) once a limit is in force. They are shown here as the script writes them.
