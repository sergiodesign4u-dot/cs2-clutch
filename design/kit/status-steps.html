Status steps

Ordered steps of something that takes time, each with whom it waits on and the time gone: done is green, now is lit, later is quiet. Everything on this page is drawn by design/system/components/status-steps.css through index.css, the same file a screen will load.

Variants and sizes

One form and one size: the list is as wide as its container (the status notice stretches it) and a step is a 13px line over a 12px line. The variation is data, the fourth axis of D-260: done, now and later are set by the product on each step as it learns where the payment is, not chosen by a designer.

  • Done (.is-done): a green dot, the step's words in white. A step that happened.
  • Now (.is-now): a pink dot with a halo, white words. The one step the person is waiting on; there is one at a time.
  • Later (no class): a ring, quiet grey words. A step that has not started.
  • Content: the name always; whom it waits on and the time gone where the product knows them. A step with no time simply leaves the third column empty.

When to use

Use it where something takes minutes or days and the person is entitled to know where it stands, who it waits on and for how long: a deposit crediting, later a withdrawal to Steam. It says what happened and what still works, in the voice's way: a name, whom it waits on, the time gone. It has no figure of the person's own and no progress percentage, because a bar of percent would claim a precision the provider does not give. The words are the voice's ("waiting on the payment provider"), never the word for a feeling.

Rule and anti-rule

Use it

Crediting on Deposit: one step now, with the time gone and the limit, and whom the money is waiting on. It stands inside the Status notice, which gives it a reference and an act.

Take another component

A single fact with its value is a Key-value line. A reason or a limit in one sentence is a Note line. A stage a person moves through by choosing is Tabs. A status mark on one thing is a Badge. An act is a Button.

States

No states: it is not interactive. Done, now and later are data the product sets on each step, not something a pointer or a keyboard does, so there is no hover, no press, no focus ring and no snapshots. The three faces are on this page at rest, in "Variants and sizes". Disabled does not apply: a step that has not started is later, and it is quiet, not dimmed.

In the code

TokenKindDarkLight

Read live from tokens.css. One component token, --steps-halo-now, for the halo round the step that is now: no role fits a pink wash at 20%. The ring of a step still to come reads the primitive --color-line directly: it is one of the six colours with one reader per surface (status steps and the popover menu) and waits for step 9. Geometry (--size-check, --space-*) and type come straight from the primitives.
