Q2. A hover and a press on Tabs and the Segmented control

kit.css draws none on a tab or a segmented option: only the chosen one is lit. The pack gives every interactive component its states, so the build added them from the state tokens: the ground lifts under the pointer (--bg-hover) and a step further when pressed (--bg-active), the chosen one does not change. Recommended: keep them, these are acts a person presses. The Text field, the Text area, the Disclosure and the Figure's link stay without a hover, as kit.css has them.

Q3. Three visible changes the rename map brings to the Text link

  • Row 20. The large link in a section head's sub line takes the family's tracking, .02em, where it drew none (the other source of the L, What still works, already has it).
  • Row 21. The two links of The algorithm published in full grow from 36px to the 40px press, as the other links with --tap have it.
  • Row 22. The Steam price link on a drop card becomes the quiet link and takes its underline.

Recommended: accept all three. They are what the rename map decided; step 6 moves the screens onto them.

Q4. The Logo in the light theme

The wordmark is white and the picture has no light form, so on the light page it reads weakly (see the Logo page in the light theme). Recommended: no new art now. Step 7 stress-tests the light theme on the whole stand, and step 8 decides whether the theme stays; if it stays, the logo gets a light form or a dark plate then.
