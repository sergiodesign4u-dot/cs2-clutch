Foundations

The values before the components: a change here costs one line and reaches everything through var(); after step 5 the same change costs every component's file and page. Architecture comes first because it holds the rules the rest is read by.

For your review, step 4

Open each page, narrow the window to a phone, switch the theme. Five things wait for your word; each answer that changes a value becomes one line in tokens.css and a row in tokens-audit.md, "Review of the foundations", before step 5 begins.

  1. The light theme's 28 new values, on Colour: proposed at step 3 (D-261), every role holding its threshold on paper. Recommended: accept them as a stress test for step 7; whether the light theme stays in the product is decided at step 8.
  2. A value declared twice: --shadow-tile-lit-soft, white at .20 for the account menu's tile and .22 for the involves plate; the second wins, so both draw .22 today (Depth). Recommended: one value, .22, what every reader shows now, so nothing on screen moves.
  3. Rarity 4 and rarity 6 at 2.9:1 on the dark tile, under the 3:1 a ring needs (Colour, contrast). It is the baseline's ladder. Recommended: keep it, since the tier is also said in words on the item, and record the shortfall as a known cost.
  4. The Figure's seven sizes, on Typography: named by the scale step each reads. Recommended: accept the names.
  5. The 22px figure, the one size off the scale. Recommended: keep it as the Figure's own value; moving it to 20 or 24 moves the price beside every act.
