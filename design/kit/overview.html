What was checked

  • Same look. Each atom was drawn twice, on kit.css and on index.css, in four states at 1440 and 360, and its computed style compared. 14 of 21 show no difference. The rest show only differences a decision names: the tile edge at .07 (D-260, Badge and Tabs), the refusal ring in the error red (rename map row 29, Text field and Text area), the Avatar's L at 52 and XL at 64 (rows 15 to 17), the category bar's chosen look moved into the option (row 12), and the three link rows and the two new states of Q2 and Q3 below.
  • The pages. All 29 pages of the stand at 1440 and 360 in both themes: no error, no failed request, no sideways scroll.
  • The snapshots. 88 images for the 14 components that have states, the Button with them, shot again and compared: 0 drifted. Eight atoms are not interactive and say so in one line.
  • No literal. No hex and no rgb() in any component file. The few colour primitives still read directly are those the audit lists as read in one block only; each file names them as waiting for step 9.

Found and fixed on the way

  • The pictures did not resolve from a component. A --art-* value was read relative to the file that used it, so the coin in icon.css looked for design/system/v4/. Each picture in art.css is now registered, so it resolves once, from art.css. No picture changed.
  • The stand painted every link pink, a component's link included. It now paints only its own words.
  • A hover on the Text field and Text area was written by the build and is taken out: kit.css draws none, and nothing is redrawn at this step.
