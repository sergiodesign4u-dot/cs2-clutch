For your review, round 2: the molecules

The 23 molecules are built, each in one pass: its file in design/system/components/, its page with the five blocks, its states in both themes, its record in this panel and in design/kit/inventory.md, its @import in the molecules' group. A molecule holds atoms and never redraws them: it places and sizes what it holds and keeps the atom's own states. Walk them in the panel on the left under Molecules: switch the theme, Tab through the live instances, narrow to a phone. The organisms wait until these questions are answered.

What was checked

  • Same look. Each molecule was drawn twice, the screens' markup on kit.css and the system's on index.css, in four states at 1440 and 360, and its computed style compared. 10 of 23 show no difference. The rest show only differences a decision names: the tile edge at .07 (row 30, D-260); the hover and press the Segmented control gained in round 1 (Amount field, Category bar); the large link's .02em tracking (row 20, Section head); the market link's underline (row 22, Drop card); the Item card's acts at the family's 14px sides (row 10; at the card's real width no label moves); the Daily tier's live act as the family's Button M (row 9, see Q3); the Money strip's Check button keeping the Button's own focus ring; and two that change nothing drawn, a mask's opaque stop written as white and a colour color-mix() returns in another notation.
  • The pages. All 52 pages of the stand at 1440 and 360 in both themes: no error, no failed request, no sideways scroll, the panel marking each page.
  • The snapshots. 86 new images for the 13 molecules that have states of their own, 174 in all with the atoms', shot again and compared. Ten molecules have no state of their own: what can be pointed at in them is an atom, and each page says so in one line.
  • No literal. No hex and no rgb() in any molecule's file; a pixel value stands only in a block's own custom property with its origin, in a breakpoint, or in a carried keyframe.

Found and fixed on the way

  • The stand outranked the molecules. Its own rules for headings, paragraphs and links weighed more than a component's class, so a molecule's title or paragraph showed the stand's size and margins. They now weigh nothing and touch only the stand's own text, so a sample draws its component and nothing else. Atom pages measured before and after: their samples now draw the component's own paragraph margins (the Prose, the Disclosure's answer), which the stand had hidden.
  • The Button's icon tile at the 44px press measured 42. .button--icon came after .button--tap and set its own size. One rule now gives the tile the 44px press; no screen wears the pair today.
  • Three keyframes were named by two blocks each (the menus' phone sheet, the outcome's band of light, the trust badge's light): each is now declared once, in the file of its first reader.
  • The card's lift under the pointer was read as a primitive by two cards; it is now the state token --depth-card-hover, in both themes, as the audit listed it for step 5.
  • Row 11 would have dropped the category bar's words. Built as written, Search and Filters lost their words and pink edge from 1200px. The bar keeps the screens' look until Q2 is answered.

Q2. Row 11 of the rename map, the category bar's two acts

Row 11 said the bar's Search and Filters become the Button's icon tile. Built that way they lose the word and the pink edge they show from 1200px, and the tile measured 42px. The screens' acts are the ghost Button M at the 44px press; the bar folds the word away under 1200px. Recommended: withdraw row 11. The acts stay the ghost M with --tap, and folding the word and sizing the glyph stay the bar's own rules, because a molecule places and sizes what it holds. This is what the Category bar page shows now: no change on screen.

Q3. Row 9, the Daily tier's act on a phone

Row 9 gives the live tier's act the family's Button M. At 360 its words no longer wrap, and "Open Guardian case" is 152px in a 139px button, spilling 13px; Silver and Legend spill at 320. The tier's name already stands above the act. Recommended: the act says "Open case" and keeps the case's name in its accessible name, "Open Nova case" (microcopy row 1.0-17 changes; the voice's rule is to shorten a string, not to re-lay the block).

Q4. A lift under the pointer

The pack's rule is that a state changes ground, colour or edge and never moves the element. Five molecules rise a few pixels under the pointer as kit.css draws them: the Daily tier 6px, the Item card 3px, the Drop card and the Recent drop card, the trust badge 2px. The rise is a transform, so nothing beside them moves, and it is motion you accepted with v4 (D-235, D-241). Recommended: keep them, named on each page as motion; stage 11 owns their timing.
