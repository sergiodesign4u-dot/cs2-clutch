Q2. The fourth state token, --opacity-disabled

The pack makes four state tokens on the reference. Three are made: --bg-hover, --bg-active and --line-focus. The fourth would have no reader. The product draws disabled once, on the Button, with its own look (D-239), and everywhere else a control stays live and refuses (D-58). Recommended: do not make it; if a component ever needs a dimmed disabled state, it is made then, in both themes, by a decision. The other choice is to make it now and redraw Exchange's disabled look as an opacity, which changes what My items shows.

Q3. The page's format for the other 69

Five blocks in this order: anatomy with a class for each part, the matrix of axes with each axis's rule and the sizes measured from the render, when to use, rule and anti-rule, states as live instances plus snapshots of named differences in both themes with the command that re-shoots them; then the tokens read live, the file, the markup, the screens. Recommended: keep the format for every component.
