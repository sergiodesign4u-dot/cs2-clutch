Logo

The product's mark, the logo art on a link's line, in two sizes: the small one in the rail, the large one in the footer. Everything on this page is drawn by design/system/components/logo.css through index.css, the same file a screen will load. The picture is --art-logo of art.css, the file design/v4/art/brand/logo2-10.svg, the founder's pick since D-209.

Variants and sizes

One axis. Its rule: the container chooses, the mark is never stretched to fit. Widths and heights below are read from the rendered marks, not from the stylesheet.

  • Size = the container. S in the rail, at the head of the strip and at the drawer's top; L in the footer. The ratio, 3.74 to 1, holds in both, so the width follows the height.
  • No colour axis. The mark is a picture and has no theme: it is the same art on the dark and the light ground (art.css). Its wordmark is white, so on the light ground, in the light theme of this page, it reads weakly: the art has no light form yet, a question for the founder and not a change made here.

When to use

The logo says whose place this is, once on each surface, at the two corners a visitor looks at first and last: the top of the rail and the foot of the page. Trust is the product (design principle 1), and a mark in the same place on every page is the cheapest sign that the page is the one the person came for. It is the mark of the product and nothing else: it is not a loading picture, a background or a decoration between sections.

Rule and anti-rule

Use it

At the top of the rail, size S, inside the link to Home; in the footer, size L. It stands on its own line with room around it, and its link, not the logo, owns the hover and the keyboard focus.

Take another component

A glyph that names a thing is an Icon. A provider's or a payment method's mark in its own colours is a picture from design/kit/marks/. A picture of a case or a skin is a Media slot. An avatar is an Avatar.

States

No states: it is not interactive. The rail's link holds the logo, and the link's hover and focus are the link's; the logo itself draws the same at rest, under the pointer and with the keyboard on it.
