Status notice

What happened to a payment, a withdrawal or a sign in, said in words with what still holds: a mark, the state as a heading, the sentences, and where they belong the steps with whom each waits on, the moment, the reference and the acts. The mark is the state; the words say the rest, so a state is never said by colour alone. Everything on this page is drawn by design/system/components/status-notice.css through index.css, the same file a screen will load.

The notice holds components and never redraws them: the Status steps (who each step waits on), the Key-value line, the Figure (a stated moment with its caption), the Copyable value (the reference, level 2), the Button (the acts), the Note line (a line beside an act) and the Icon (the mark's glyph), all level 1 but the Copyable value. The notice's own part is the plate, the mark tile and its tone, the body, the heading, the sentences and the act line; it stretches the steps, the reference and a key-value line to the body's width.

Variants and sizes

One axis of data, the tone, and no size: the notice takes its column's width and its words keep their measure. Sizes are read from the render.

  • Tone = where the thing stands. In progress (--progress): it is moving and someone is on it, the steps say who; the striped blue tile. Stopped (--stopped, the base): it did not go through or is closed, the sentences say why and what still works; the plain tile. Failed (--failed): it broke on our side or the other's, with a reference to quote; the red wash. The tone is in the markup the page or pay.js writes, never inferred from colour.

When to use

Where something the person started did not end as they meant, or has not ended yet: a payment crediting, declined or held by a limit, a sign in Steam would not confirm. A stop says what happened and what still works (the voice, D-166): who the wait is on, the moment a limit resets, the reference support will ask for. It never sells, and a limit's moment is a stated time, never a countdown.

Rule and anti-rule

Use it

Deposit, crediting: in progress, the one step and who it waits on. Sign in, Steam refused: failed, the fault named as ours, the reference with Copy, Try again and Support.

Take another component

A refusal beside one control is a Note line under it. A record of a payment that went through is the Payment record. A screen with nothing to show is the Empty state. The limits a person set are the Limits panel.

States

No states: it is not interactive. Its tone is in the markup it arrives with, and what it holds keeps its own (Copy and the acts press as the Button, the reference answers as the Copyable value).
