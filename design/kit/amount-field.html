Amount field

A label, the amount with its unit and the presets that set it: the card route's amount in Add funds. It is one column that holds a Text field and a Segmented control and adds only its label, the weight of the figure and the wrap of the presets. Everything on this page is drawn by design/system/components/amount-field.css through index.css, the same file a screen will load.

Variants and sizes

One form: it has no variant, no size and no emphasis. The size of each part is the atom's, read below from the rendered sample, not from the stylesheet; the container sets only the width.

  • Width = the container's. The column fills the room it is given. At 360 the six presets wrap to a second row; the amount stays one line.
  • Presets = the route's. The number and the figures of the presets belong to the route, not to the molecule. A route with no presets draws the label and the amount alone.

When to use

The amount field is where a person says how much goes in, so the unit stands beside the figure and the presets only set it: the person sees "$40" before the act that spends it (design principle 3, clarity at every risk moment). A preset is pressed while the amount is its figure, so what the presets say and what the field says never part. The label says what the figure is and in which unit, in the voice's plain words.

Rule and anti-rule

Use it

Deposit, the card route: "Amount, in US dollars", the figure in the field, six presets under it that set it.

Take another component

A number only shown is a Figure. One choice among a few with no typed figure, such as how many cases to open, is a Segmented control. A single typed value with no presets, such as a promo code, is a Text field in a Field. A limit set in Responsible play is the Limits panel's row, not this molecule.

States

No states: it is not interactive. The column has no hover, press or focus of its own, because each of its states belongs to what it holds: the Text field's pink ring inside the box on focus, and the Segmented control's hover, press, focus and chosen option. They are on the Text field's page and the Segmented control's page, so no snapshot is repeated here.
