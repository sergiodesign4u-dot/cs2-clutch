Typography

One family, Roboto, in four weights, with 900 doing what a display face would (D-182, D-183). Every sample on this page is a line the product says, from voice/docs/microcopy.md, set by the system's own tokens.

The family

Loaded by each page's link to Google Fonts and named through --font-family (). base.css sets it on .page, with the page's size --size-s and a line height of 1.55; nothing else in base.css sets type.

The Figure's seven sizes

The Figure's value is sized by the container it stands in, at seven sizes (D-260 point 5 left their names to this page). Six of them already read a step of the scale above; one, 22px, is a literal in two components. Proposed: each size is named by the step it reads, and 22px stays the Figure's own value, since moving it to 20 or 24 would move the price beside every act. The names are yours to change; nothing is renamed before step 5.

Line height and measure

Every case shows the chance and current value of each item, plus tested RTP and expected value at that cost. Withdrawals go to Steam, commission free. Close the Steam tab and press Sign in again. Nothing you chose is lost.

Body text runs at 1.55, the line height .page sets. Headings run tight, from 1 to 1.4 (DESIGN.md, Hierarchy), set in their components. Prose stops at --size-measure, , the measure the grey screens fixed at stage 04.
