The grid holds a molecule and an atom and never redraws them: the Item card (level 2), one in each cell, and the Segmented control (level 1, .seg--s, the sort keys). On My items' other states it holds the Empty state (level 2) in place of the list, and a Note line (level 1, .note--soft .note--label) over it. The grid's own part is the bar with its title and the sort's label, the list, and how it places the cards.

Variants and sizes

One form; its content and its width change it. Sizes below are read from the render.

  • Columns = the width. One card to a row under 600px of window (the founder's pick of D-232 over the grey's two); from 600px as many as fit at --igrid-min, 176px, the drop card's smallest. A card here: .
  • Content = the page. My items carries the sort keys; a public profile (grey so far, wireframes/player.html) carries the title alone and cards with no tick and no acts. With nothing held the list gives way to the Empty state; with values that cannot be read a Note line stands over the list, and those cards say Not available.

When to use

Where a person's items stand together as a shelf to look through and act on: My items, and the public shelf of a profile. Each item is a card in its tier, with its value; the order is the person's to choose by the two keys, Newest first and Highest value, and pressing the pressed key flips its order and its words (D-89).

Rule and anti-rule

Use it

My items: All items, sorted Newest first, the cards ticked for the Selection bar under the grid.

Take another component

What a case can drop is the Drop card's table, not this grid. Cases to open stand in the Case grid. The best drops of a case scroll in Recent drops. One past round with its history is the ledger's row, not a shelf.

States

No states: it is not interactive. The sort's keys are the Segmented control's and keep its hover, press and ring. items.js sets what changes: aria-pressed and data-dir on the keys and their words (Newest first, Oldest first; Highest value, Lowest value), and the order of the list; the scripts join the system at step 8. A card's tick, acts and sale are the Item card's.

In the code

Read live from tokens.css. Two roles, the title's ink and the label's; no component token, no colour primitive. Geometry from the primitives and the grid's own --igrid-min.
