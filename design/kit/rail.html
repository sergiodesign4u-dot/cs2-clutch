Variants and sizes

One form. What changes is its state, set by kit.js, and each row's destination. Sizes are read from the render below.

  • Form = one. The logo, Cases, the heading, the eight not launched in a fixed order, the foot. A way to play that launches becomes a link in its row (D-197); the rail never grows a second form.
  • State = set by kit.js. From 900px: open beside the page (no class, the page layout widens its column, .shell.is-wide), closed to its marks (.is-closed), or open over the page with the page dimmed (.is-overlay) where the product column is 1440px or less. Under 900px it is the drawer, hidden until the header's burger opens it (.is-open). The current page's row carries aria-current="page".
  • Row = live or not launched, and its destination. Cases is the one live row. The eight carry --later and their destination's modifier, which picks the card's fill and the object that rises out of it.

When to use

The rail stands on every screen, once, and does not change on sign in (D-40). It carries the destinations, the ways to play, and nothing of money or the account, which the header carries. A carrier is inherited whole and only its dead items are deferred: the eight ways to play that are not launched keep their rows and say so, rather than leaving the rail with one row (CLAUDE.md, "Baseline"). On a phone the bottom bar carries a shortcut subset of it, Home and Cases, and the rail is always the superset.

Rule and anti-rule

Use it

The left of every screen: the logo home, Cases, the eight not launched under their heading, the sound and the language, Social. Mark the current page's row with aria-current="page".

Take another component

Money and the account are the Header's and the Account menu's, never a row. Provably fair and Responsible play are carried by the Footer. On a phone the two shortcuts are the Bottom bar. A way to play on a page is a Mode card.

States

Live at rest in the frames below: point at the rows and reach them with Tab. Open, closed, over the page and the drawer are set by kit.js (the classes above, and aria-expanded with the words "Expand the menu" or "Collapse the menu" on the toggle); the scripts join the system at step 8, so they are shown as the classes the script sets. The frames carry .is-open, which kit.js sets under 900px and no rule reads from 900px, so from 900px they draw the open rail and under 900px the open drawer.

  • Closed: a row's name and a foot tile's name come out beside the panel as a chip under the pointer and for the keyboard, wiped out of the tile; the language's flag alone stands on its tile and its list opens beside it (the Popover menu's --fit).
  • No press face in kit.css, and none is added: a row goes to its page. The sound tile and the language keep the Button's and the Popover menu's own states. The rail has no disabled look (D-58): a row not launched says so instead.
  • The stories of the toggle's lines, the speaker and the flag play under the pointer with motion allowed; with reduced motion they do not, so the snapshots show the lines at rest. With motion allowed, the toggle's lines turn to the white mark, --bg-mark, while they run.
