Media slot

One framed area for a picture, with its aspect: a skin floats on whatever it lies on, a case fills its picture and lets the skin lie in its tray. Everything on this page is drawn by design/system/components/media-slot.css through index.css, the same file a screen will load.

Variants and sizes

One axis, content, and no size of its own: the slot has the width of the container that holds it and takes its height from its aspect.

  • Skin = a thing on a card. A skin floats: no plate, no clipping, the image fitted whole. Its frame, the tier's edge and the plate under it belong to the card around it (the item card, the feed tile, the withdraw item), which also draws the skin's shadow and its tilt under the pointer.
  • Case = a case with its skin. 3 : 2, the picture of the case behind, the skin tilted across its tray, and the front of the case cut at the rim over it. The case's name and colour are data set by the screen.
  • Width = the layout. The slot never sets its own width, as the Button never does.

Left to right: a skin on its card's plate, a case with a skin and the default light, a case with a skin and its own light (blue), a case with no skin, which draws no front copy.

The frame, with its aspects

The three aspect modifiers on the default frame, for the grey pages that will need them; no coloured screen wears them yet.

When to use

A media slot holds a picture of a thing the player can win or open: a skin, a case, a mode's art, the clip of a story. It carries no words and no state; the name, the price and the tier are the card's. Where the drop is shown, the skin is shown as it is, whole and uncropped, because what is won must be what is seen (design principle 1).

Rule and anti-rule

Use it

The skin on an item card and in the live-drops ticker, the case on a case tile and in the case head. A picture that is data, in a frame the container sizes.

Take another component

A person's picture is an Avatar. The product's own mark is the Logo. A glyph is an Icon. A picture that is a button is a Button with a label, never a slot with a click. A card's frame, edge and plate are the card's, not the slot's.

States

No states: it is not interactive. The skin that straightens and rises under the pointer on an item card is that card's own rule on the picture inside the slot, so the card's page shows it.

In the code

TokenKindDarkLight

Read live from tokens.css. One component token, --media-case-glow, the case's light when its screen names none; no role is a light colour. The slot's ground is --bg-menu, the role named for it, and the skin's shadow --depth-skin-drop. No colour primitive is read directly. The shape of the case's front is --shape-case-rim, a primitive; the blur of the case's light, 10px, is a custom property of the block (--media-case-blur), a literal in kit.css.
