Variants and sizes

One form, no modifier. What changes is the data, never the component: the lines are the payment's, the total is the line that decides last, the acts are the ones that apply while it waits. The widths below are read from the render.

  • Lines = the payment's facts, in the order a player asks them: how much, by what, when, and the line that decides last (the minimum to send to Steam). Each is a Key-value line at M (D-260 row 24: the old rule on .record__lines > .kv is the atom's size).
  • The total stands last, and under a hairline. It is the Key-value line's own --total; the record writes no rule of its own for it.
  • Width = a card's, not the column's. The plate stops at 520px; the note and the acts run the container's width.

When to use

A payment record is for a payment that is already made and not yet done: the deposit on its way, the same record on a support case when it is upheld. It says what the payment is, which line decides when the money can leave, and when to ask for help ("Not there after {n} minutes? Support answers within {n} hours", microcopy 4.1-64). It is a reading, never a form: nothing here is edited, and the money is the glossary's (a deposit is one payment, C4).

Rule and anti-rule

Use it

Deposit, once the payment is paid and credited: the amount, the method, when it started, the minimum to send to Steam, and the ways on.

Take another component

A sum of what is about to be paid, beside the form, is the Deposit dock. One value the player copies is a Copyable value. Two or three facts of a drop are Key-value lines in a card. A step in a flow is Status steps. A stop with a reason is a notice.

States

No states: it is not interactive. The lines are read, never pressed; the Buttons and the Text link carry their own hover, focus-visible and press (Button, Text link).
