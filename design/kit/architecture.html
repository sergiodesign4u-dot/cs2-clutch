Architecture

How the system is built, read before anything else on the stand. The source is design/kit/docs/architecture.md; this page is its visible place and changes with it.

Two ladders, both called levels

They are different axes. One says where a value comes from; the other says what a component contains.

The ladder of tokens: where a value comes from

1
Primitive

A raw value with its origin beside it, carried from kit.css line for line: colours, space, radii, sizes, type, motion, shadows, gradients, shapes, icons. Pictures live in art.css (D-259).

2
Semantic

A role a component reads: a job that repeats in two blocks or more, may part from its neighbour, and paints one surface. Always a var() of a primitive, with its readers, parent and contrast in both themes.

A component token is made only at step 5, only where a state finds no role. A job two components or more repeat is a role even when the components bring it: the atoms brought four, --line-act, --line-lit, --line-control, --bg-error (D-264); the molecules five, --line-focus-on-plate, --text-meta-on-plate, --bg-scrim-sheet, --bg-handle, --depth-menu (D-265).

Roles and their names

The prefix names the surface a role paints, because each surface has its own threshold (WCAG 1.4.3 and 1.4.11). A role on one surface never paints another, even at the same value: a fill on a 12px label passes as a fill and fails as text.

The suffix -on-plate marks the same job on a coloured plate or a picture instead of the theme's ground. The theme moves the ground and leaves the plate, so the two part in the light theme, as above.

What a component reads

  • Colour only through the semantic level. The theme and a rebrand override colour; a component that reads a colour primitive is a hole the first theme finds. Named exception: a colour read in one block only stays a primitive until step 9 decides.
  • Geometry, type and motion straight from the primitives. A radius, a gap or a duration has nothing to override.
  • No literal. No hex and no pixel value in a component's file. A state reads a state token, --bg-hover, --bg-active, --line-focus, made at step 5 in both themes, or, where it lands on no role, a component token (--button-bg-disabled) made by the founder's decision at the round's gate (D-263). There is no opacity for disabled: disabled is drawn once, on the Button, and a control otherwise stays live and refuses (D-58).

The themes

The product is dark from its first day (design principle 4, D-258). :root holds the dark roles and [data-theme="dark"] repeats them, so a part of a page can stay dark inside a light one. [data-theme="light"] overrides the semantic level only, never a primitive. Every role has its pair, and a pair is not a mirror: contrast is measured against the opposite ground, so the light theme takes another step of a colour, or a new value named "variable, value, why" in tokens-audit.md.

Two folders

design/system/: what the product loads

A package that can be lifted out whole.

  • tokens.css: primitive, semantic, the light pair
  • base.css: the reset, the page's type and ground, the shell's layout
  • art.css: the pictures
  • index.css, from step 5: tokens, base, art, then @import by level
  • components/: one file per component
  • screens/: a screen's own blocks, one file per screen (D-259)

design/kit/: what shows the system to a person

Never loaded by a screen.

  • _nav.js: the one registry of the stand, panel and cards; _page.css: the stand's styles; _tokens.js: reads tokens.css for these pages
  • overview.html: the hub
  • a page per foundation and, from step 5, per component
  • docs/: the audit, the census, the architecture; the inventory stays at design/kit/inventory.md (D-258)

A product class in the stand, or a stand style in a component's file, is a defect.

Why @import goes by level

@import "tokens.css";  @import "base.css";  @import "art.css";
/* atoms */      @import "components/button.css";  ...
/* molecules */  @import "components/case-tile.css";  ...
/* organisms */  @import "components/field.css";  ...  @import "components/dialog.css";
/* screens */    @import "screens/home.css";  ...

Bottom up: a container loads after what it holds, so the cascade lets a container adjust its parts and never the other way round. Alphabetical order would load the Button after the Dialog that styles it.

Adding a component: five things at once

  1. design/system/components/<name>.css, its states in both themes.
  2. design/kit/<name>.html, its page with five blocks.
  3. A record in design/kit/_nav.js, in the group of its level.
  4. A row in design/kit/inventory.md with its level.
  5. An @import in its level's group in index.css, never at the end of the file.

A card on the hub is not a sixth: the registry draws it.
