The dock holds five components and never redraws them: the Figure (the sum, .fig--money at .fig--head, level 1), the Key-value line (what is charged, .kv--total, level 1), the Note line (the fee line; the refusal above the act, level 1), the Field (the terms row, .field--declare with its Checkbox, level 2) and the Button (the act, primary at L, level 1). The dock's own part is the plate, the receive block with its head, its peg chip, the bonus and the cap, the total's block and the act's cell, and how it places them: the act across the dock's width, the terms row at the Field's padding.

Variants and sizes

One axis of data and no size: the route fills the dock and the dock takes its column's width. Widths and heights are read from the render, at the 320px the Payment panel gives the dock from 900px; under 900px the dock follows the form at the form's width.

  • Route = what the person pays with. A card: the sum and its bonus at the peg, the total and the fee line, the terms row and Pay. Crypto: the rate on the chip with when it was read, the bonus as a promise, and the act that says the coins were sent; the person sends what they send, so no sum is drawn. Skins: the chip counts the skins, the sum their value, the act adds them.
  • Width = the column's. The dock never sets its own width; the act fills it.

When to use

Where money goes in. The dock answers before the press what arrives, at which peg, with which bonus and its cap, and what the card is charged, so cost never hides inside the act (design principle 3, the coin's peg printed wherever money is spent, D-28). It carries the one primary act of the deposit, and that act refuses in words above itself when something is missing (D-58).

Rule and anti-rule

Use it

Deposit's card route: the dock beside the form from 900px, sticky as the form scrolls, under the form on a phone; the same dock in the Add funds dialog.

Take another component

Spending coins on a case is the Commit block, not a dock. A payment that already happened is the Payment record. A balance in the header is the Money strip. A deposit that stopped or waits is the Status notice over the form.

States

No states under the pointer: the dock is not interactive. What it holds keeps its own (the terms row's link and its ticked and missing faces are the Field's, the box the Checkbox's, the act the Button's). One state is set by the product's script: when Pay is pressed and something is missing, pay.js writes the refusal as a .note.note--refuse above the act and marks the terms row .is-missing; the scripts join the system at step 8, so it is shown here as it writes it.
