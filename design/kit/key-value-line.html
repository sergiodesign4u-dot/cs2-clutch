Variants and sizes

Four axes. The sizes below are read from the rendered lines, not from the stylesheet.

  • Emphasis = how loud the line is in its container. The record line is plain and quiet, a row among rows (a payment, a limit's rule). The fact is a line a card is read by, Chance and Tickets on a drop: bold white, its key larger and soft so the pair reads from a glance (rename map row 23).
  • Content = what the line holds. A line is a pair. A total carries a hairline over it and a step more size, the one that decides last. A money total is the same line with its value in gold. A rule holds a sentence for value, on a fixed key column.
  • Size = the density of the container. S 12 is the line; M 14 is the payment record's lines, written in a larger run than a card's facts (row 24). A total is 14 by its own rule.
  • Ground = what the line stands on. The theme's ground is the default: in the light theme the key and the value turn to dark ink. A plate that keeps its colour in both themes (the Proof block's dark hash well on the fair blue) wears .kv--on-plate: the same look as the dark theme draws, kept in light (D-266). It covers the line, the rule, a missing value and money; the total's hairline and the fact have no on-plate role and stand on no plate, so they are not worn with it.

The fact's padding above and its end alignment are its own (the card's facts hang at the foot of the card). A container with a tier sets --kv-tint to the tier's colour on the cell that carries it, and the value takes 35 % of it into its white: the drop card's tier cell. With no tint the value is plain white.

When to use

A key-value line is for facts a player compares: the chance and ticket range of a drop, the lines of a payment, the amount that arrives. The key says what is measured, the value says how much, in the same row, so the eye does not hop between a label and a number placed apart. The numbers are the published ones, and a value that cannot be known is said in words, never as a zero (D-124).

Rule and anti-rule

Use it

Chance and Tickets on a drop card, as .kv--fact. The payment record's Total charged, as .kv--total. You receive on a cash out, as .kv--money.

Take another component

A number alone, with its caption below it, is a Figure. A line that says something, not a pair, is a Note line. A value a person copies is a Copyable value, which holds a Key-value line and the Button that copies it. A sentence is Prose.

States

No states: it is not interactive. A line is read, never pressed; a value to copy is the Copyable value's, and its Button carries the states.
