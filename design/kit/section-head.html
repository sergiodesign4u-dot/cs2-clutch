Section head

A section's title on a striped tile in a mode's colour with the section's mark, then a line in that colour fading out, and a sub line under it or beside it. Everything on this page is drawn by design/system/components/section-head.css through index.css, the same file a screen will load.

Variants and sizes

Two axes. Each carries its rule, so the next screen picks by the rule. Sizes are read from the render.

  • Size = the head's job. M is a section's head (20px). L is Featured cases' larger title on Home and a screen's H1 where the screen has no head block of its own (Deposit, D-237); 26px, and 21px under 600px (D-234).
  • Data = the mode the section belongs to. Cases is the default, pink to violet. Upgrade is amber, Battles blue, Showdowns red; the colour is a modifier and the mark is an Icon in the tile. The colour names the section's mode and never a state.
  • Content = optional parts. A sub line, an onward link in it, a count beside the title. A head with none of them is the title and its tile.

When to use

One head to a section of a page, naming what the section is in the words of the screen: "What is in this case", "Best drops", "Published against observed". It gives the page its outline (the screen reader's headings) and its rhythm, and the mode's colour tells which part of the product the section belongs to without a legend. The sub line says what the section shows in one short sentence, with the way on if there is one; it never carries a claim the section does not prove, which is design principle 1, trust is the product.

Rule and anti-rule

Use it

Over a block of a page: Best drops over the strip of recent drops, with "By value" and the link to what usually drops. One H1 per page: where a screen has no head block, its head is the L.

Take another component

A label inside a panel or over a field is a plain label, not a head. A path to the page is a Breadcrumb. A head that belongs to a page and carries art is the page's own head block (the catalogue's, the fair page's). A destination is a Text link, which the sub line holds.

States

No states: it is not interactive. The head is read, never pressed. The one thing in it that can be pointed at is the onward link, and that is the Text link's own hover and focus (its page); the head draws none instead of it.

In the code

TokenKindDarkLight

Read live from tokens.css. Geometry, type and tracking come straight from the primitives. Three colours are read directly and wait for step 9: --color-head-upgrade, --color-head-battles and --color-head-showdowns, the line of a head in its mode's colour, read in this block only. The head's four own sizes (--sec-head-tile-s, -icon-s, -line, -l-s) are written in the file with their origin, as kit.css writes them. The sub line's link at 16px is the Text link's --l, with the family's .02em tracking (D-264): the screens' rule drew it at normal tracking, a difference of 0.32px per letter that is the one change from what they draw.
