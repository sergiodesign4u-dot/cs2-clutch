Case grid

A shelf of cases: the section's head, the cases as tiles, and on Home the act that leads to the whole catalogue. The shelf lays its tiles out by its own width. Everything on this page is drawn by design/system/components/case-grid.css through index.css.

The grid holds molecules and an atom and never redraws them: the Section head (level 2, .sec-head--l), the Case tile (level 2, one in each cell, with its Favourite toggle) and, on Home, the Button (level 1, All cases). The grid's own part is the list, the cells, the foot and how it places them.

When to use

Wherever cases stand together to be chosen and opened: Home's Featured cases with the way to all of them, and each section of the catalogue. Every tile carries the case's price and its risk at the moment of choosing, so cost never hides in the excitement (design principle 3).

Rule and anti-rule

Use it

Home: Featured cases, five a row and All cases under them. The catalogue: one grid per section, four a row.

Take another component

The free daily cases by tier are the Daily ladder. Items a person holds are the Item grid. What a case can drop is the Drop card's table. The four ways to play are the Mode row.

States

No states: it is not interactive. A tile's link, its tip and its Favourite toggle keep their own states. On the catalogue a filter hides what does not match: design/kit/catalogue.js sets hidden on a cell, and on a whole section when nothing in it is left, and the grid draws nothing for it (.case-grid[hidden], .case-grid__cell[hidden]). The scripts join the system at step 8.
