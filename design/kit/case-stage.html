Case stage

Where the case stands, the open runs and the prize lands: one region in three phases that never unmounts (case.md section 0). At rest the case stands large over a band of the items it can pay; the open runs the band to a line; the outcome puts the prize in its place. Everything on this page is drawn by design/system/components/case-stage.css through index.css, the same file a screen will load.

At rest, one case. The stand frame wears the Case head's navy, which on the screen the Case head gives the stage.

The stage holds four components and never redraws them: the Media slot (level 1, the case at rest, .media--case), the Outcome card (level 2, one per prize at the outcome), the Button (level 1, a batch prize's Sell, .button--ghost .button--s, which the Outcome card places) and the Rare mark (level 1, laid on a run's card by the script). Its own part is everything that moves the open: the band and the columns the cards run in, the run's card (.case-stage__card, the stage's own, as the live drop's card), the two pointers and the line the runs stop on, the lines between the cells, the rest that holds the cases, and the win with its light. It writes on what it holds only to place it: the case's width by the count, the coin's size in a card's value, where a card puts its Rare mark, and the Outcome card's chance and Sell rising in one after another after the landing.

Variants and sizes

One axis of count, one of data and the phone's size. Sizes are read from the rendered stage and cards, not from the stylesheet.

  • Count = how many cases one press opens, 1 to 5 (data-count, the Segmented control of the Commit block sets it). One stands over the band; two to five part the stage into cells, the band folds shut and a column opens behind each case. The stage keeps one height whatever the count.
  • Tier = the best of the landing (.case-stage--r1 to --r7, set by the script): the light the prizes stand on, the ring they land with and the colour the pointers turn to as a run slows. A run card's tier is its item's (.case-stage__card--r1 to --r7); Restricted and Mil-Spec are lit a step on the dark stage (--bg-rarity-4-lit, -5-lit).
  • Size = the phone's or not. Under 900px the band and the cards step down (cards 120 x 136); under 600px the stage is 250 high, the cards 96 x 112, the pointers 28 x 24, and at the outcome it grows to hold its prizes (348 for one, 320 for two or three, 520 for four or five in two rows).

When to use

To open a case where the person can watch it happen: the case they chose, the items it can pay running past, the line the run stops on, and what came out, in one place that never leaves the screen. The reveal is the product's job, so the motion carries it (design principle 2), and it must read as honest: the result is settled before anything moves, the run only shows what was picked, no near miss is drawn beside the stop, and whoever asked for less motion gets the same result in the same order, stilled (case.md 7.1 to 7.3, principle 1). The values on the cards are samples (D-124); a value that cannot be read is not drawn.

Rule and anti-rule

Use it

The case screen, inside the Case head, above the Commit block that spends: one stage per case screen, its count set by the Commit block's count, its phase by the open.

Take another component

Listing what a case can pay, with chances and tickets, is the Data table of Drop cards. One prize on its own is an Outcome card. A case to choose is a Case tile. The live drops of everyone are the Ticker.

States

No pointer or keyboard state of its own: nothing in the stage takes the pointer or focus but a batch prize's Sell, which is the Button's, shot on its own page. Its phases are set by the product's script, design/kit/stage.js, which this page does not run; each is shown below as a still frame of the attribute or class it sets. The cards drift and the pointers bob at rest; reduced motion stops them, the band standing a quarter along and the light under the prizes shown at once.

In the code

TokenKindDarkLight

Read live from tokens.css. No component token: every part found its role. The pointers are the act's pink, --bg-act; the line, the band's lit edges and the landing ring are --line-lit; the white in the card's ring and the pointers is --bg-mark-on-plate. The run card's edge is --line-edge, white at .07 where kit.css drew .05 (merged drift, D-260 Q1). Geometry and motion come from the primitives and the block's --stage-* custom properties, each with its origin. Colours read directly, all waiting for step 9: --gradient-stage-band, --gradient-stage-edge, --gradient-stage-cell, --color-pointer-deep, --shadow-case-stage (read in this block only), and the hit's --gradient-stage-bam and --shadow-stage-wave (elements the script adds, read by this block only).

File: design/system/components/case-stage.css, loaded by design/system/index.css in the organisms' group. Its play is design/kit/stage.js (and motion.js for the Rare mark on its cards), which moves into the system at step 8.
