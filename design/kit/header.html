Header

The strip across the top of every screen. It owns the money and the account and holds no destination: a guest sees Sign in; signed in, the balance and the value of items held on one dark plate, each the link to its own place and never summed (D-28), Add funds with its bonus, then the Account menu. From 900px the pink light falls from its top and weapons fall in it; under 900px the burger that opens the rail's drawer leads it. Everything on this page is drawn by design/system/components/header.css through index.css, the same file a screen will load.

Variants and sizes

  • Layout = the screen's width. From 900px the strip is sticky at the window's top, lit pink from its top with the weapons falling in the light, its left kept for the rail's toggle. When to use

The header stands at the top of every screen, once, and changes only between guest and signed in. It carries money and the account, the two things a person checks wherever they are, and nothing else: no way to play and no page of the product is a link here, because the rail and the bottom bar carry the destinations and no carrier holds another's kind (CLAUDE.md, "Baseline"). The money is never a bar item and the two figures are never summed (D-28).

Rule and anti-rule

Use it

The top of every screen in both states: Sign in for a guest; for a signed-in person the balance and the value of items held, Add funds with its bonus and the account's pill.

Take another component

A destination (Cases, a way to play, Provably fair) is a row of the Rail, a shortcut of the Bottom bar or a link of the Footer, never a header item. The account's own places open from the Account menu. A screen's own band with the money and the tabs, as My items draws it, is the Account head. Money in a block of a page is the Figure or the Money strip.

States

The header's own controls are the two figure links and, under 900px, the burger. Live at rest in the frames above: reach the figures with Tab. The held components keep their own states, shown on their pages: the Button's Sign in and Add funds, the Account menu's pill and the menu it opens.
