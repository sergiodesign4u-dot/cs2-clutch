Sign-in block

The two declarations, each its own row and its own act (D-26), the refusal under them (D-58), Sign in with Steam, the three other providers, and the foot plate that answers the fear and says what Steam is needed for. No press is disabled: a press with a declaration missing marks that row, names it in the line under the rows and takes the keyboard to it. Everything on this page is drawn by design/system/components/signin-block.css through index.css, the same file a screen will load.

Variants and sizes

No modifier and no size of its own: the block takes its column's width. Two axes are said by where it stands and what it holds. Sizes are read from the render.

  • Carrier = where the person met it. On the page (/signin) the block is followed by the page form, .signin__away, so a person who came by link has a way on without an account; in the Dialog the close and the scrim are the way out, so it has none (node 2.4, D-56, D-251).
  • Width = the dialog's container, not the window. Inside a Dialog of 760px or more (the sign in dialog's two columns) the block reads the container named dialog and takes more air: 22px over the act, 20 and 16 round the rule, 20 under the providers, the foot plate at the column's foot, the declarations as the Field's roomy row (.field--roomy). Anywhere narrower, the one column.
  • Failure = Steam answered badly, or not at all. A Status notice stands in .signin__fail where the act and the providers were, and the declarations stay in view under it: failed with a reference (Steam refused), stopped (Steam not answering).

When to use

Wherever a guest is asked to sign in: the page /signin and the sign in dialog every guest screen opens, a favourite or an act for an account included (D-237). It carries the two declarations of the age and the terms as two rows and two acts, never one box for both (D-26), and the act stays live so the refusal can say what is missing (D-58). The foot plate says what Steam is needed for and what it never asks, because that is the fear the research recorded.

Rule and anti-rule

Use it

Sign in, cold arrival: the page form, both rows empty, no standing line. Sign in, a press with the age row empty: the row takes the refusal ring, the line says "The age declaration is still missing." and the keyboard is on that row.

Take another component

A declaration beside a payment is the Field's declaration inside the Deposit dock. A plain yes or no in a layer is the Dialog's plain form. What happened after Steam answered, on its own, is the Status notice. A single tick with no act is the Checkbox.

States

The block has no hover, focus or press of its own: each belongs to what it holds (the rows as the Field and its Checkbox, the acts as the Button). Its states are set by kit.js ("the sign in declarations, D-58"): a press with a declaration missing puts .is-missing on the row (the Field's refusal ring and a short nudge), aria-invalid="true" and aria-describedby on its box, the refusal in .signin__why as a .note--refuse, and the keyboard on the first row missing; both ticked, the line says "Both declarations made." The scripts join the system at step 8, so below the block is live at rest and its three set states are twins carrying what the script writes.

In the code

TokenKindDarkLight

Read live from tokens.css. No component token and no colour primitive read directly. The foot plate is the note plate (--bg-note-plate) with its hairline (--line-edge), as the Status notice's; the plain tile a wash (--bg-wash) with the body ink; the guard tile the stripes on the blue (--bg-stripes-s, --bg-mode-battles) and the Steam tile Steam's blue (--bg-steam), both plates that keep their colour in both themes, so their glyph reads --text-on-plate. The tile's size (--signin-tile, 30px) and the spacing that changes in two columns (--signin-go-above, --signin-or-above, --signin-or-below, --signin-note-above and their wide pairs) are declared on .signin with their origin.

File: design/system/components/signin-block.css, loaded by design/system/index.css in the organisms' group, after status-notice.css and before dialog.css, whose container its five container rules read.
