Variants and sizes

One axis, content. Sizes are read from the render. The table answers to the window: the drop table is a grid of cards from 600px and one card a row under it; the ledger is a table of bands from 600px and each row a card of label and value lines under it, each label read from the cell's data-l. This page at 1440 shows the wide form; open it at 360 to see the phone form.

  • The drop table (--drops, D-125): every item in the case with its value, its chance as a percentage and its ticket range, grouped by tier, the stamp under it. Design principle 1: what the round resolves against is printed.
  • The ledger (--ledger): a history or a settlement, one row per event, its state with the reason beside it, never in a tooltip (B8-3). It stands on no coloured screen yet; it waits for the history, node 5.9, at the rollout.
  • A row marked mismatched (data-state="mismatch", set by the product): our recomputation of a round does not match what we published; the row says so in words and offers the way on. No verified mark anywhere: a matching proof is said in words too.
  • Empty: a ledger with no rows gives way to an Empty state that says what would put a row here.

No cash out yet

When you cash items out to a wallet, the row lands here and stays, with the network, the wallet, the amount and where the request got to.

When to use

A data table stands where a player needs every row to compare and to check: what is in a case before opening it (the case screen, and the case shown in Deposit and Sign in), and what happened to their money and their rounds afterwards (history and settlement, at the rollout). Every chance and every value is printed, never summed into a score.

Rule and anti-rule

Use it

"What is in this case": every item as a Drop card, by tier, with the stamp. A history of deposits, withdrawals or rounds, a row per event with its state and reason.

Take another component

The best drops of a case by value are Recent drops. Published chances against observed rates are the case screen's own block. A few figures a player weighs before spending are the Money strip. One payment on its way is a Payment record. A grid of a player's own items is the Item grid.

States

No states: it is not interactive. A row marked mismatched is data the product sets (data-state="mismatch"), shown above. What it holds keeps its own states: point at a Drop card and it lifts (Drop card), a link takes its own ring.

In the code

TokenKindDarkLight

Read live from tokens.css. Geometry and type come straight from the primitives; the one pixel literal no primitive equals, the narrowest card of the grid, is a custom property of the block (--data-table-card, 176px) with its origin in the file. One component token, saying why no role fits: --data-table-row-edge. A mismatched row reads the roles --bg-error-wash, shared with the Account menu's Sign out, and --line-error, a refusal's ring: its red moved from --color-down to --color-error by D-266 (Q1), as row 29 moved the fields' ring. The ledger's row card under 600px takes the tile edge at white .07, the merged drift of step 3 (row 30).
