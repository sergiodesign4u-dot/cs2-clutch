Daily ladder

The five tiers of Daily cases: each tier is a case, its name and its status, under a head with the title, the reset and, for a guest, the one act. It is a table of tiers and not a climb (D-220): the ladder never counts down to the next tier. Everything on this page is drawn by design/system/components/daily-ladder.css through index.css, the same file a screen will load.

Variants and sizes

Three axes, and each carries its rule. A limit or a free case never gets a climb, a streak or a score (CLAUDE.md, Compliance; D-220): none of the three below counts toward anything.

  • Audience = what the person can do. .daily--guest: every tier says the wager that opens it and one act, Sign in, sits in the head. .daily--acct: the head shows the wager so far and the reset, and each tier is reached, opened today or locked. The account markup is the guest's with another head and other chips.
  • Tier = a fixed data axis of five. Each tier has its hue, plate and case, and none is more important than another.
  • Tier status (an account) = what the tier can do now. .is-live: the one tier whose case opens now, higher, with an edge of its hue and the open control inside it. --opened: already opened today. .is-locked with --locked: not reached, its case darkened. A script sets the state the open leaves behind: the tier is swapped for the Opened today chip and the say line is written. It is drawn here as the markup that script leaves.
  • Size = the panel's own width, never the window's. .daily is a container, so a panel in a narrow column lays itself out like a phone.

When to use

Use it where a person is told that a case is theirs once a day and at what wager each tier opens: Home and the top of the catalogue. It says the number and not the superlative (the voice): "At 100.00 coins wagered", "Opened today", "Open Nova case". A tier gives a case and nothing else. Its parent is the daily free case, which sits in the MVP by a founder decision with no parent in the three legal classes (CLAUDE.md, Scope); it carries that cost in the open, and D-220 took the climb out so the ladder does not become the completion mechanic the Compliance rule forbids.

Rule and anti-rule

Use it

Home's "Daily cases": five tiers, the head with the reset, the guest's one act, an account's one live tier. The tiers are a table: read the wager, read the status, open the case that is live.

Take another component

A grid of cases to choose from is a Case tile in the catalogue, not a tier. One card with a figure and a link is a Mode card. A banner for an event is the Event banner. A count towards the next tier, a streak or a score is not a variant of this ladder: it is refused (D-220).

States

Live at rest, on this page's theme: point at a tier. The open control inside the live tier is the Button's: its hover, press and focus ring are on its page and the molecule writes none of its own for it.

Snapshots of the same two instances, dark on the left and light on the right, taken with reduced motion so each shows the face the state settles on. The base set is shot once, on a plain tier, and after it only the live tier, which is the one named difference.

  • Hover: a tier rises 6px and its edge brightens, its drop deepens and a glow in its own hue stands round it (--daily-tier-lit-hover, --daily-tier-lift-hover). Under reduced motion it does not rise and keeps the brighter edge, drop and glow, so the snapshots show no rise on a plain tier.
  • Live tier hover: it rises further (12px to 18px) with the deeper drop, and keeps its edge of its own hue (--daily-tier-lift-live-hover). The live tier's rise is kept under reduced motion, as kit.css has it.
  • The same answer, said once: a tier is not a control, so it has no focus ring and no press; the one control inside it is the Button. A locked tier is not disabled: it says what it takes (D-58).
  • The skins of the live tier lift and settle (daily-lift, 2.6s); under reduced motion they stay still.
