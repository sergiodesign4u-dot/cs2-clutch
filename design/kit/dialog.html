Dialog

One layer over the page with a close, a heading and its content; the page behind is dimmed by the scrim and inert. Ways out: the close, a press on the scrim, Escape. On open the keyboard goes to the close and Tab walks the layer's own controls only; on close it returns to the control that opened it (D-251). At 360 it is a sheet from edge to edge, from 600px a card with its drop. Everything on this page is drawn by design/system/components/dialog.css through index.css, the same file a screen will load.

The layer is fixed to the window, so it is shown inside a stand frame that holds it: the frame is a page's window, the layer fills it. The plain form, Responsible play's confirm.

Variants and sizes

One axis, the content, and the window chooses the shape: under 600px a sheet from edge to edge, from 600px a card; the sign in form takes its two columns where the card has 760px (the container dialog), a window of 808px. Sizes are read from the render.

  • Content = what the layer asks. Sign in (--signin): the art, the three lines and the Sign-in block. A plain question (--plain): a heading, the sentences, what changes and the acts, for a confirm (Responsible play), Cash out (My items) and, in the grey, the geo gate, which has no close: its acts are its ways out. Add funds (--pay): the Payment panel. The filters (--drawer): at the right edge, the window's height, the whole window on a phone.
  • Carrier = a layer or the page. The layer (.dialog__layer) floats over a screen. On /signin the same sign in dialog is drawn on the page: no close, no scrim, its heading the page's H1, and the Sign-in block's page form under it (node 2.4, D-251).

When to use

Where the person must answer one thing before going on, and the page behind stays as it was: sign in from a guest screen, Add funds from any money act, a confirm before a limit or a self exclusion starts, Cash out, the filters on a phone. Each says what it changes and what stays open, and has a way out that costs nothing (the close, the scrim, Escape, or Cancel). It never opens on its own over a page the person did not ask about, but for the geo gate, which answers where they are.

Rule and anti-rule

Use it

Responsible play, Start self exclusion: the plain form with what closes, what stays open, that it cannot be ended early, Cancel and Start. A guest presses a favourite: the sign in form opens over the page and the keyboard is on its close.

Take another component

A short list under a control is the Popover menu. The account's actions are the Account menu. What happened to a payment, on the page, is the Status notice. A choice the page can hold in place is a Field or a Disclosure, not a layer.

States

Open and closed are set by kit.js ("layers"): [hidden] on .dialog__layer while closed, taken off by a press on any data-layer naming it, put back by the close, a press on the scrim or Escape; a layer drawn open on arrival (deposit-dialog.html, signin-dialog.html) takes the keyboard as a press would. The scripts join the system at step 8, so here the layer is drawn open, in a frame, and closed is the same markup with hidden, which draws nothing. The Dialog has no hover, focus or press of its own: the close and the acts are the Button's, the content's are its components'.
