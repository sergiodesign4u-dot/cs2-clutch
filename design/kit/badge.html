Badge

Short text in a pill: a bonus pinned to an act, a ribbon on a tile, a status, a count beside a title. One shape for every badge; the tone changes the colour and nothing else. Everything on this page is drawn by design/system/components/badge.css through index.css, the same file a screen will load.

Variants and sizes

Two axes. Heights and widths below are read from the rendered badges, not from the stylesheet.

  • Tone = what the badge says. Bonus (the unmarked badge) is an offer, status a quiet fact about a thing, count a number of things, good an agreement, bad a mismatch. The tone never changes the size, the case or the spacing (D-230).

When to use

A badge labels a thing that is already there: a bonus on an act, a coin on a method, a number of items. It is a few words or a number, never a sentence, and it is not a control. Its words state a fact and do not shout: "No fee" is a fact about the card total, not a superlative (the voice, principle 2). Bad carries one rule more: it says our recomputation did not match, so it is never used for the user's own mistake, which is a refusal beside its field (Note line).

Rule and anti-rule

Use it

"No fee" in green on a payment method, and the count of the user's items beside the title of My items. A few words or a number, the same shape in every tone, placed by its host.

Take another component

A word that acts is a Button or a Text link. A refusal beside a field is a Note line. A choice between a few is a Segmented control or Tabs. A figure with a unit is a Figure. A badge is not pressed and does not carry a sentence.

States

No states: it is not interactive. A badge is read, never pressed; the one pinned to a Button is part of the Button's act, and the Button's own states cover it.
