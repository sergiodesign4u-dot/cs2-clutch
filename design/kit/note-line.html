Note line

One small line that qualifies a block. The refusal beside a control says what is missing; the stamp says as of when. Everything on this page is drawn by design/system/components/note-line.css through index.css, the same file a screen will load.

Variants and sizes

Two axes, and the refusal is a tone that carries its own form. Sizes below are read from the rendered lines.

  • Tone = what the line is doing. Muted is the plain qualifier. Soft is the same line one step quieter beside a title. Hi is an answer the player just got, in white (a saved limit). The refusal is the line beside a control that says what is missing: one line in a light error red with a round "!", no box and no side stripe (D-231), and the control stays live (D-58).
  • Size = the container's text size. S 12 is the default. M 14 is the label's size (.note--label). L 16 is the line under a block of the case screen, in the base ink at the page's text size (.note--l, rename map row 26). Its measure and the gap above it belong to the container.
  • Content = a fact, a stamp or a label. A stamp says as of when and sets its figures in columns.

When to use

A note line qualifies the block above it with one sentence. It carries the facts a player should have beside a figure and does not ask for attention: the stamp says as of when, the qualifier says what a number does not. The refusal is the line a control shows when it cannot do what its label says: it says what is missing and what still works, and the control stays live (D-58). The words are the voice's, a straight answer rather than a word for a feeling.

Rule and anti-rule

Use it

"Credited at our value for each skin once Steam completes the trade." under a route. A refusal beside the Verify form's hash field. A saved limit's answer after a press.

Take another component

A paragraph or more is Prose. A line that is a fact as a pair (a key and its value) is a Key-value line. A notice that something went wrong with the whole action, with its reference and what to do, is a Status notice. A count or a status mark is a Badge.

States

No states: it is not interactive. A note is read, never pressed. A refusal appears when a control refuses and goes when the cause does; the line itself has no hover or focus of its own.
