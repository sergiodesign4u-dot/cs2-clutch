Field

A label and its control with the words around it: the label over the control, the act under it, the line that says what happened; a limit's words at the left with its control at the right; a declaration that is its own press. Everything on this page is drawn by design/system/components/field.css through index.css, the same file a screen will load.

Anatomy

A nonce is a whole number. This one has a decimal point in it.

Redeem

The Field holds atoms and never redraws them: the Text field (.input), the Popover menu in its select form (.popover--select), the Text area (.textarea), the Checkbox (.check, the declaration's box), the Switch (.switch), the Button (an act under the control) and the Note line (.note, .note--refuse), all level 1. The Field's own part is the label, the row's words and its control's cell, the declaration's row with its sentence and links, and the act line; it sets the gap between them and leaves each control's states to the control.

Variants and sizes

Two axes. Sizes below are read from the rendered fields, not from the stylesheet.

  • Form = what the control asks. Stacked (the default) where a person types or picks a value: the label over it, the note under it. Row (--row) where a setting stands among others and its words explain it: a limit, a sound. Declaration (--declare) where the person states something and the row itself is the press (D-26, D-58).
  • Size = the container. M everywhere; roomy (--roomy) only in a dialog that has its two columns, 760px of dialog and more, where the declaration grows to 58px and its sentence to 15px. Outside such a dialog --roomy draws M.

Size

The same two declarations in a frame named as the Dialog's container. At 760px of frame and more they are roomy; narrow the window under it and they fall back to M, as they do in the sign in dialog. The frame is the stand's own (.cp-dialog): the query reads the Dialog, an organism of its own.

When to use

A Field is how a control meets its words: every typed value and every pick carries a label a screen reader hears, and every refusal stands under the control it names, in words that say what is missing and what still works (D-58). The declarations of sign in are two separate rows and no press goes through until both are made (D-26, D-58); the dock's terms row gates the one act that pays. A limit is a row: its name and what it does at the left, its amount, its period and its act at the right, so the four limits read as one list and never as a dashboard.

Rule and anti-rule

Use it

The Verify form's four inputs, each a stacked Field with its label and, when it is wrong, the reason under it. The sign in dialog's two declarations, each a row of its own, ticked one by one.

Take another component

An amount with its unit and presets is the Amount field. A value to copy and not to type is the Copyable value. A line of label and figure that is read, not set, is the Key-value line. A choice among a few, all visible, is the Segmented control; a filter in force is the Filter chip.

States

Live at rest, on this page's theme: point at the links, reach them with Tab, tick the boxes. A declaration named as missing (.is-missing) is set by the product's scripts, kit.js in sign in and pay.js on the dock, after a press that found it unticked; the scripts join the system at step 8, so it is shown as a twin carrying the class the script sets. The ticked row is the box's own :checked.

Snapshots of the same instances, dark on the left and light on the right, taken with reduced motion so each shows the face the state settles on (the nudge of a missing row does not run).

  • The controls keep their own. The Text field and the Text area ring pink on focus and red under aria-invalid, the select form opens and rings, the box lights its ring under the pointer, the Switch slides: those are the atoms' states, shot on their pages, and the Field adds none on top of them. The row and the stacked field draw no hover and no press.
  • No disabled look. A Field stays live and refuses with its reason under it (D-58).
