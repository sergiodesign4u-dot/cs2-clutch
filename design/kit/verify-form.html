Verify form

Paste a round and recompute it: the server seed hash and the server seed as text areas, the client seed and the nonce, the act, and the one area each recomputation replaces. No state draws a verified mark; the words say what agreed. Everything on this page is drawn by design/system/components/verify-form.css through index.css, the same file a screen will load.

The form holds components and never redraws them: the Field (each input stacked with its label, level 2) with the Text area and the Text field, the Button (Recompute it yourself, primary at M; Report this round), the Key-value line (the matched results), the Copyable value (the reference) and the Note line (a line under a result; a refusal under a field), all level 1 but the Field. The form's own part is the plate, the act line, the result area in the hash well with its sentence, and the two values side by side when they disagree.

Variants and sizes

One axis of data: what the recomputation found. The form takes its column's width (3 of 5 parts of Check a round from 900px, the whole column under it); the two values stand side by side from 600px of window. Sizes are read from the render.

  • Result = what the recomputation found. Empty or prefilled: no result area. Malformed: each field that failed says why under itself, every failure at once, nothing typed cleared. Matched: the sentence and the results as Key-value lines. Failed: the two values side by side, the fault named as ours, one report act with the round attached and a reference. Unavailable: no proof yet, and why. Never a tick, a seal or a green mark on a match (Provably fair, 0.14).

When to use

Where a person checks a round themselves, without an account. Trust is the product (design principle 1): the form lets anyone recompute what we published and says in words whether it agreed. A failure is ours to explain, so the failed result names the fault as ours and gives one way to report it with a reference, never a verdict drawn as a mark.

Rule and anti-rule

Use it

Provably fair, Check a round: the form at the left, what a round proof is made of at its right; opened from a result with the round in the address, the form arrives prefilled and computes nothing until pressed.

Take another component

Showing a round's proof read only is the Proof fields. A seed to copy is the Copyable value. A sign in or a payment that stopped is the Status notice.

States

No states under the pointer: the form is not interactive. What it holds keeps its own (each control's focus and refusal ring, the act's press, Copy). The results above are set by the product's script, fair.js, which joins the system at step 8: on a press it writes each field's refusal under it and sets aria-invalid on its control, and it replaces the result area whole; the form's own line ([data-fair-say], a .note--refuse) shows only when it has words, by hidden. They are shown in Variants as the script writes them.
