When to use

The Case head stands once, at the top of a case's page: it says which case this is (the page's H1), how to get back and how the case works, and it is the ground the reveal plays on, so the open happens in the same place the case was named (design principle 2, the reveal that feels real). Deposit and Sign in open as layers over it and leave it in place.

Rule and anti-rule

Use it

The head of one case's page with its stage under it.

Take another component

A case in a list is a Case tile in a Case grid. A page head on the fair blue or the catalogue's head is the screen's own head. Who an account is at the top of an account page is the Account head. The week's event across Home is the Event banner.

States

No states: it is not interactive. What it holds keeps its own: the Favourite toggle (hover, focus-visible, pressed and its burst), the Breadcrumb's links and How it works, a Text link (hover, focus-visible). The stage's phases (the open, the outcome) are set by stage.js on the Case stage, never on this block.

In the code

TokenKindDarkLight

Read live from tokens.css. The block stays dark in both themes, as the stage it carries (D-266, Q3), so it reads roles that keep their value: the base ink, the name's white, its inner hairline (--line-edge-on-plate, shared with the Footer, the run card and the Copyable value's chip). Three colours read in this block only stay primitives until step 9: the navy --color-stage, its fall --shadow-stage and the name's lift --shadow-case-name. No component token. The three pixel values no primitive equals are the block's custom properties with their origin in the file: --case-head-name, --case-head-inset-l, --case-head-page.

In the light theme the block, its name, its trail and the stage stay as in dark (the trail's --fit already reads on-plate inks). Three held atoms read inks written for paper and part from the dark look; the head may not restyle them, so they go to step 7 with the plates of D-266: the Note line's soft grey turns dark, 1.7:1 on the navy where dark reads 11.8:1; How it works, the Text link, turns to the deep pink, 3.1:1 where dark reads 5.4:1 (both measured from the computed colours on --color-stage); the Favourite toggle's square turns to a white veil with a dark heart.
