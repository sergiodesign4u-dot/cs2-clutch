Geometry and motion

Space shown as space and a corner as a corner, the sizes of controls and of the shell, where the layout breaks, and the motion the product carries today. Geometry is read straight from the primitives: a gap has nothing a theme would override.

Space

The seven steps of direction 2 (--space-1 to --space-7, 4 to 56px, D-187) and the half steps the molecules needed (D-231). Each drawn as the gap it makes between two tiles.

Corners

Rounded and soft, sharp nowhere. Cards, tiles and buttons take one corner, 12px (D-209, D-218); a circle is a control or an avatar; a pill is a chip.

Sizes of things

Every size that is not a type size, from a hairline to the content column, drawn to scale up to the width of this column. No target in the product is smaller than --size-tap, 44px.
