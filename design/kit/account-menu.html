Account menu

The avatar pill in the header and the menu it opens: who is signed in with the ID and Copy, My items first, four destinations, Responsible play and Sign out. Under 900px the menu rises from the bottom as a sheet. Split from the Popover menu by its head (D-260, Q3). Everything on this page is drawn by design/system/components/account-menu.css through index.css, the same file a screen will load.

When to use

The account menu is the one place a signed-in person reaches their own things from any screen: My items first, because the items held are what the product gives back, then History, Withdrawals, Profile and Settings, then Responsible play, then Sign out. It sits in the header beside the money, never in the rail, so no carrier holds another's kind. Its words are the destinations' names, the same as the account's tabs say them.

Rule and anti-rule

Use it

The header of every signed-in screen: the pill with the avatar and the name, opening the menu with the ID to copy, My items first and Sign out at the foot.

Take another component

A short choice of a value (a language, a country, a period) is the Popover menu, which has no head. The account's own pages switch between each other by Tabs. A value to copy on a page is the Copyable value. A way to play is a row of the rail, not a tile here.

States

Live at rest, on this page's theme: point at them and reach them with Tab. Open, Copied and Not copied are set by kit.js (aria-expanded on the pill, .is-done and .is-fail on Copy), and the scripts join the system at step 8, so they are shown as twins carrying the value the script sets.

In the code

TokenKindDarkLight

Read live from tokens.css. No component token. A tile that turns into a plate (My items, Responsible play, a tile under the pointer or on focus) carries its words in --text-on-plate, the same white in the dark theme, so the light theme keeps white on the pink and the blue. The phone sheet reads the roles --bg-scrim-sheet, --bg-handle and --depth-menu, shared with the Popover menu (D-265). Sign out under the pointer reads the role --bg-error-wash, shared with the Data table, and Not copied the roles --text-fail and --bg-fail-wash, shared with the Status notice (D-266, Q1). Read directly and waiting for step 9: Copy's --color-done and --color-done-wash, set by a script and so missing from the step 3 count. Geometry, type and motion come straight from the primitives.
