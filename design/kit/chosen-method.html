Chosen method

The head of a route at step 2 of Add funds: Back to the ways to pay, the method's mark, and its name under its family (D-238). On a route's own page the name is the H1. Everything on this page is drawn by design/system/components/chosen-method.css through index.css, the same file a screen will load.

Variants and sizes

One form. The only axis is the mark's content, and it changes the picture, not the molecule. The mark's height is read from the rendered sample, not from the stylesheet.

  • Content = the mark's picture. A wide mark (Visa or Mastercard, 124 by 40), a narrow one (Bitcoin, 60 by 40), or .chosen__mark--skin where the route is skins: a skin is drawn half again as tall in its transparent square so the rifle reads at the plates' size.
  • Size = the window's. Under 600px the mark steps down to 32px and the name to --size-title, so the name keeps its room. Narrow the window to see it.

When to use

A route's page says which way to pay the person chose and gives the way back: the mark and the name confirm the choice, the family over it says which group it came from, and Back returns to the ways to pay without losing the place (D-238). The bonus, the code and the country stay at step 1, so this head carries nothing else. The name is the method's own, as the provider writes it.

Rule and anti-rule

Use it

The top of a deposit route: "Crypto, Bitcoin" with Back at its start, over the route's own form.

Take another component

A heading with no way back and no mark is a Section head. Choosing among the ways to pay is the provider tiles of the Deposit screen's own block, not this head. A path of pages over the whole site is the Breadcrumb. An act that does something is a Button.

States

No states: it is not interactive. The row has no hover, press or focus of its own; the Back tile is a Button and keeps the Button's hover, focus-visible ring and press (the Button's page), so no snapshot is repeated here.

Read live from tokens.css. The molecule reads two ink roles, --text-body for the family and --text-hi for the name; the Back tile reads the Button's.
