Avatar

A player's Steam picture in a circle with a hairline ring; a bot has no picture and draws an empty disc. Four sizes by the container, one circle. Everything on this page is drawn by design/system/components/avatar.css through index.css, the same file a screen will load.

Variants and sizes

Two axes. Diameters below are read from the rendered circles, not from the stylesheet.

  • Size = the container's density. S 16 in the live-drops ticker, M 32 on the header's account trigger (the default), L 52 over a best drop card, XL 64 at the head of the account page and of the account menu. By D-260 the old L (64) is renamed XL (row 16) and the best drop card's own 52 becomes the new L (row 15); the account menu's head, which sized the avatar itself, takes XL (row 17).
  • Data = who is shown. A player has a picture; a bot has none, and its disc stays empty on purpose, so a bot is not dressed up as a person.

When to use

An avatar says who: the player who won a drop, the account that is signed in. It is a person's mark and nothing else; it carries no status, no level and no count. A bot is shown as a bot, an empty disc, never a made-up face.

Rule and anti-rule

Use it

The winner's picture in a live drop, and the signed-in account's picture on the header and at the head of My items. One circle, one size from the container's density.

Take another component

A skin or a case is a Media slot, never a circle. The product's own mark is the Logo. A glyph is an Icon. A badge on a person is a Badge beside the avatar, not drawn into it.

States

No states: it is not interactive. An avatar that leads to a profile is wrapped by a Text link, which has its own states; the circle itself never changes under the pointer.

In the code

TokenKindDarkLight

Read live from tokens.css. One component token, --avatar-bg-bot, for the bot's disc, which no fill role holds; its ring is the role --line-ring-soft, with its own light value. The large ring is --depth-avatar. Three colours are read in this block only, --color-raised, --shadow-avatar and --shadow-avatar-s, and wait for step 9. The diameters are --size-avatar-s, --size-avatar-m and the block's own --avatar-l (52) and --avatar-xl (64), declared on their modifiers.
