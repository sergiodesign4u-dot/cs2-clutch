Mode row

The four ways to play on Home, one live and three not launched (D-197, D-27). The row reads its own width, not the window's, and asks its cards for the side or the stacked form. Everything on this page is drawn by design/system/components/mode-row.css through index.css.

The row holds one molecule and never redraws it: the Mode card (level 2), four of them in their fitted form, .mode-card--fit, the card's own variant that reads this row's container (mode-row) to take the stacked form where the row is narrow. The live card's act is the Button (level 1), held by the card. The row's own part is the heading for a screen reader, the grid and the room it leaves over the cards for their art.

PartClass or elementWhat to change there
The row.mode-row (a <section>, the container mode-row)the width its cards read; its own --mode-row-gap

Variants and sizes

One axis, the row's own width; the window does not decide it. The frames below are the row at four widths, measured from the render.

  • From 992px of row: four side cards (words left, art right).
  • 624 to 991: four stacked cards (the art over the words).
  • 516 to 623: two side cards a row, two rows.
  • Under 516: two stacked cards a row, 12px apart.

When to use

On Home, once, after the event and before the cases: the ways to play this product will have. The baseline's row of four came back by founder decision D-27; one is live and three say they are not launched (D-197), so the row is the carrier inherited and filled with its live item (CLAUDE.md, "Baseline").

Rule and anti-rule

Use it

Home: Cases with its act Open a case; Case battles, Showdowns and Upgrade saying Not launched yet.

Take another component

Cases to open are the Case grid's tiles. The rail is where a person moves between ways to play on every page (the Rail). A single event is the Event banner.

States

No states: it is not interactive. The live card's act is the Button's, and the card's lift and art under the pointer are the Mode card's. No script sets a state on the row.

In the code

TokenKindDarkLight

Read live from tokens.css. One role, the base ink the cards' plain words inherit; no component token, no colour primitive. Geometry from the primitives and the row's own --mode-row-gap.
