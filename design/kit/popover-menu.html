Popover menu

A control that opens a short list over the page: the language at the rail's foot, and the Select menu that stands in for the browser's own list (the country, the network, a limit's period). Everything on this page is drawn by design/system/components/popover-menu.css through index.css, the same file a screen will load.

Anatomy

Only English is available for now.

It holds no component on the coloured screens, so it is an atom (D-265, Q5): every part below is its own. The step-2 inventory listed Button, Text link and Icon; those were the account form's (its close and its glyphs) and left with it for the Account menu at the split (D-260, Q3).

Variants and sizes

One axis, the content, and one fit to the cell. Each carries its rule. Sizes below are read from the rendered controls, not from the stylesheet.

  • Content = what is chosen. --lang: the nine languages, one live and eight that say so under them (D-41, D-42). --select: a field's choice from a short list, the country, the crypto network, a limit's period (D-238); the former native Select, merged here (D-260, row 1).
  • Direction = where the room is. The language stands at the page's foot and opens upward; a select stands in a form and drops under its field at the field's width. The panel never decides its own width: the language list is 320px, the select's is its field's.
  • Fit = the cell, not the menu. --fit fills the rail's cell; in a cell under 60px (the closed rail) it is the flag alone on a square tile. The cell is the rail's, so the rule is a container query.

When to use

A popover menu picks one value from a short, closed list without leaving the page: a language, a country, a network, a period. The chosen value stays on the trigger, so the person reads what is set before opening it. A choice that is not live yet still stands in the list and the note says so, "Only English is available for now." (D-42): the control does what its label says or names the absence beside it.

Rule and anti-rule

Use it

Deposit's "Payment methods for": the country's flag and name on the field, the list dropping under it with the chosen one ticked. Or the rail's language tile opening the nine languages upward.

Take another component

The account's destinations are the Account menu, which has a head this menu lacks. Two to four choices seen at once are a Segmented control or Tabs. A choice that shows its consequence beside it (a provider) is a Radio in a tile. Going somewhere is a Text link.

States

Live at rest, on this page's theme: point at them and reach them with Tab. Open is set by kit.js on the trigger's aria-expanded, and the scripts join the system at step 8, so a press here does not open the list: the open form is shown as a twin carrying aria-expanded="true", the value the product sets.
