Variants and sizes

One axis, the content. The card has no size of its own: it fills the width its container gives it and stacks inside it. The heights below are read from the rendered cards.

  • Nothing yet (the default): a list, a ledger or a shelf with nothing in it. The mark tile, the heading, one sentence on how it fills, one primary act. On My items.
  • Nothing matches: the filters leave nothing. No mark; the heading says it, the sentence names the filters, the ways to widen each carry their act, and the count of the whole stands last. On the catalogue.
  • Gone (--gone): taken down or never there. The heading is larger and in capitals. No coloured screen wears it yet; step 6 verifies it against the grey's result and player pages (D-260).

When to use

An empty state stands where a list, a shelf or a page has nothing to show, and it always says three things: what is not here, why, and what to do next. It does not apologise and it does not blame: "No items yet." (microcopy 5.1-36), "Nothing matches these two filters." (3.1-26). Where the player can widen the search, each way to widen carries its own act and the count of the whole stands under them, so a filter never ends in a dead page. The voice's rule applies: say what is true and what still works.

Rule and anti-rule

Use it

My items before the first open: "No items yet.", one sentence, "See the cases". The catalogue when two filters meet nothing: the sentence names both, each way to widen is an act.

Take another component

A single line that qualifies a block ("Not available") is a Note line. A refusal beside a control is the Note line's refusal form. A value nobody has, inside a row, is the Figure's missing form or a Key-value line's. A failed block or a stopped service is a notice with its reason, not an empty state.

States

No states: it is not interactive. The card is read, never pressed; the Button and the Text link inside carry their own hover, focus-visible and press (Button, Text link).
