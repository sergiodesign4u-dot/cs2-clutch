Everything on this page is drawn by design/system/components/payment-panel.css through index.css, the same file a screen will load.

Variants and sizes

No modifier: the panel's form is said by its step and its route, the panes it shows. Widths come from the window (two columns from 900px) and from the column it stands in; sizes are read from the render.

  • Step = how far the person is. Step 1 shows the strip, the head and the ways to pay; step 2 shows one route and nothing of step 1 but the dialog's name for a screen reader. On a route's own page the panel is step 2 only; in the Add funds dialog it holds every pane and pay.js shows one (D-100, D-238).
  • Route = how the money comes in. Card: providers, the amount, the e-mail, the facts, the dock. Crypto: the network, the address with its code, the facts, the dock at the rate. Crypto with no wallet: the address's place says so and offers to make one. Gift cards: the sellers as Disclosures with their amounts, and the code to redeem at the side. Skins: the skins to tick, the dock counting them.
  • Notice = a payment that did not go as meant. Declined: a stopped Status notice between the head and the form, the form kept. A raise pending: the notice in progress with its moment, the form inside the limit in force.

When to use

Wherever money comes in: Add funds from the header, from a price the balance does not cover, from the account head, and Deposit's own pages. It says the peg and the bonus before anything is chosen, keeps the code and the country beside the ways to pay, and on a route keeps what arrives, what is charged and the one act in view beside the form (design principle 3, D-28). A refusal is said above the act in words, never by a dead button (D-58).

Rule and anti-rule

Use it

Deposit, card: the route's head, two providers with one chosen, the amount at $40.00, the e-mail, the facts, the dock with Pay $40.00. Deposit, declined: the stopped notice between the head and the form, the form as it was.

Take another component

Money going out to a wallet is the Dialog's plain form (Cash out). A payment that went through is the Payment record. Only what arrives and the act, with no form, is the Deposit dock. The limits a person set are the Limits panel.

States

The panel's own interactive part is the provider tile. Live at rest, on this page's theme: point at a tile, reach its radio with Tab, choose one. The rest belongs to what it holds (the code, the amount, the presets, the dock's declaration and Pay keep their component's states). The step is set by pay.js: [hidden] on .pay-panel__offer, .pay-panel__head, .pay-panel__pick and each .pay-panel__route (the panel's [hidden] rule takes it out of the flow), shown above as the two steps. pay.js joins the system at step 8. The strip's light and coins move only without reduced motion; with it, they stand still.

Chosen, a twin

Snapshots of the same tiles, dark on the left and light on the right, taken with reduced motion.

  • Chosen is the radio's own :checked, the person's press, not a class: the tile takes the Cases mode's pink to violet and the radio on it turns white, ring and dot.
  • Focus-visible lands on the radio's input; the whole tile takes the ring, --line-focus, and the radio inside keeps its own.
  • Hover, on a tile not chosen, lifts it a step (2px, --size-lift) with the act's pink edge, as kit.css draws it; a chosen tile does not move. No press face is drawn, and none is added.
