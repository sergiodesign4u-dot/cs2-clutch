Round 3, what changed

  • One card for every row: Cases had the button's stripes and the hover cards did not. The stripes are the language of a button, the cards on Home have none, so no row has them now.
  • The marks: at rest every mark has the menu's own grey, the same as its word, not the colour of its mode. A mark turns white when its row is lit: Cases always, any other row under the pointer.
  • Cases breaks out of its card as the Showdowns helmet does under the pointer: this week's case stands on the card's floor and rises above its top edge. The list starts a little lower to give it room.
  • Sound and language are back to the two tiles of the current rail, side by side, stacked when closed.
  • B2: dropped, the founder took B1.
  • The logo: tried here in round 3 and taken out in round 4: the logo lives in the header, where Home now carries logo 10.

Round 4, what changed

  • The closed rail's name: the grey plate beside a closed row read as a stock tooltip. It is now the row's own card coming out of the rail: its mode colour, its name, and for Cases this week's case, for the eight the words Not launched yet and the mode's object.
  • Case battles: its hover sends up the two crossed daggers of its mode card, not one knife.

Round 5, what changed

  • The closed rail's chip stands beside its tile with a gap, its own rounded shape, rather than joining the tile into one strip.
  • Stripes are back on every card: Cases, each card under the pointer and the chip, the same faint stripes as Home's buttons.

Round 2, what changed

  • A, C and D: dropped, the founder took B.
  • B's live row: 74px tall, 18px type and a case standing out of it, so it read as a banner rather than the chosen row. It now has the rows' 48px and 14px.
  • The icon: 12px from the edge and 4px inside the coloured card. It now sits 20px from the edge, 12px inside the card, the same column in every row and in the foot.
  • The closed rail: not drawn in round 2. Drawn here at 70px, the width the theme already gives it.

  • Every direction: with the sentence gone the eight rows looked live, so it came back, once, as their heading.
