My items head

The founder on D-241: What is the idea, to add a top skin? Visually nothing changed. I do not even know; let's make variants. D-241's idea was to put the account's own best skin on the band. Placed small beside the money, it read as one more object on the same band, and the composition stayed the same. So here are three different compositions of the same head. Each keeps everything the head has to say: the picture, the name, the ID, the Steam account, the balance and the value of items held, never summed, Add funds with its 5%, and the four tabs. Each uses only the head's existing words and the kit's existing values.

The cards under the head are D-241's, each in its grade, and stay whichever head is chosen. Narrow the window to see each direction on a phone.

Updated after publication, 21 Nov 2026. The founder picked B1: Carry B1 over. It is now on My items itself (D-242): see My items. This lab stays as the record of the choice.

Round 2: B refined

The founder chose B: Let's refine this one. What round 1's B left weak: the hexagon under the skin read as a dark patch the gun hid, the skin stayed inside the band instead of coming out of it, and the band was one flat gradient. Three ways to stage the skin follow. All three share one new touch: the foil that runs across the outcome card when a case opens now runs across the skin's own shape every few seconds. Under each stage a switch shows it with other grades and with nothing held, since the band takes the grade of the best item and changes with it.

B1. Breaking outRecommended

No plinth. The skin is larger than the band and comes out over its top and its right end, the way the mode objects on Home come out of their cards. Only a soft contact shadow sits under it. The capitals of its name stay behind it across the band. This is the plainest of the three and the one that reads first as one big object. After the founder's look at round 2 the gun is smaller, about three quarters of the size, and the price chip stands over it rather than under it.

Cost: the skin rises about 60px into the breadcrumb's row on the right. A short gun such as a pistol rises less and looks smaller, as the switch shows.

Round 1

A. Wallet tilesRecommended

The head becomes the Home's Ways to play. The account sits on one line at the left: the picture, the name, the ID and the Steam account. Beside it stand three saturated tiles, each a figure with its own object breaking out over its top and breathing as the mode cards do. Balance is gold, with the Daily Legend case full of coins and Add funds at its foot. Value of items held is pink, with the most valuable skin and a line naming it. Steam is Steam's blue, with its mark, the account, a live green dot and the way to Settings. On a phone the tiles go two by two and Steam takes the width.

References: SkinBeast's profile stat row (behance.net/gallery/239810777); the rewards cards of Goated (behance.net/gallery/258296119); our own mode cards on Home, which the founder accepted.

Cost: the money is still two figures, never summed, but each now has a tile of its own, so the head grows by about 80px on a desktop and 200px on a phone. The Daily Legend case is a picture reused from Home. The Steam tile repeats the inventory head's Steam card, which would be removed if this direction is chosen.

B. Hero stage

The most valuable item held becomes the head itself. The band darkens to the left and takes the skin's grade on its right. The skin stands there at 640px, larger than anywhere else on the site, on its hexagon. Its name runs behind it in capitals across the whole band, and a chip under it says what it is. The account's name is set in heavy italic capitals at the left, over the money plate. On a phone the skin comes first and the name and money follow.

References: Jetskins' showcase card, one skin large on a slant with its name in display capitals (behance.net/gallery/178657043); CSGOLuck's welcome card (behance.net/gallery/239049851); the founder's own cs2-promo, one real skin as the hero with huge type behind it.

Cost: the band's colour depends on the best item's grade, so the head changes colour from account to account and after a sale. With nothing held it needs its own empty form. It is the tallest head of the three.

C. The collection

The band keeps its pink, and everything held stands along its foot: every skin in a row, turned and overlapping, from the most valuable down, each with its value on a chip edged in its grade. Under them runs one bar of the value held, split by grade, so the share of each item can be read at a glance. The money plate and Add funds stay at the top right. On a phone the row shows the four most valuable items.

References: Jetskins' wall of cases and skins as objects (behance.net/gallery/178657043); Lumpbox's team value bars (behance.net/gallery/257303301).

Cost: the row repeats the grid two rows lower. A long inventory has to be cut to a number of items, and the bar can be read as a score if it is ever labelled as one, so it carries no label but the value held.
