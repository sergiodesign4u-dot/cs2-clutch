Rare skins in the band

Founder of 6 Nov 2026: a rare skin in the band carries a mark of its own, "plasma burning or lightning", without the frame rate falling. Rare here is rare in this case, not a tier: "the rarity in a loot box can depend on the box, somewhere there can be a rare Mil-Spec". So a skin is rare when its chance in this case is at or under a threshold you set. Four ways to draw the mark, from the cheapest up, and a counter of frames per second; switch a way on, let the band drift or spin, and press Measure. The counter is this machine's; a phone will run lower.
