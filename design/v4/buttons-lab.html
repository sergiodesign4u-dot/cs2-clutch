Buttons

One radius of 10px. Large 52px, medium 40px, small 32px. Use .v4-btn with --l, --m or --s, and --ghost for the quiet kind: a dark fill with the 2px pink edge, which fills with pink under the pointer. .v4-ibtn is the icon button, the side menu's 42px tile that turns pink under the pointer, as the footer's social squares; .v4-ibtn--close is the close, a white cross on a dark see-through tile that turns red and rotates under the pointer, as the sign in dialog's. Under the pointer nothing moves: a band of light crosses a filled button once and its glow appears.

Contrast, before and after

White on our pink is 3.4:1, and 3.1:1 on the lighter stripe. A 13px label needs 4.5:1 for WCAG AA, and only a label of 18.7px bold or larger may stop at 3:1, so every pink button on Home falls short. Each row computes its own figures from the colours it draws: the worse of the two stripe bands decides. The founder picked A on 4 November 2026, and every filled button on Home now draws it, from --color-button in buttons.css; the other rows stay as the record.
