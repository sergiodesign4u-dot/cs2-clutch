Rare drop

Picked on 1 November. A rare drop arrives once, the way every drop arrives in the live drops, turning in from the side; then it lands with a hit and a wave. After that it travels down the strip and keeps a look of its own, set by the chance it dropped with: lightning from 1 to 5%, a golden orbit or an ice rift from 0.1 to 1% (two candidates), plasma under 0.1%. Each strip below is the live drops at their real size, 104px cards between the header and the banner, 8px from each, so no effect reaches more than 8px off its card. The skin is real, Karambit Tiger Tooth. The chance on the card is a sample.

Chance on the card
Crown on the prize card

1 to 5%: lightning

Short discharges crawl along all four edges and change their fork as they go; the edge under each lights up, and a strong one flashes into the card.

0.1 to 1%, candidate one: golden orbit

A comet of gold light runs round the frame with a fading tail, a fainter one opposite it, and throws sparks off at each corner; behind the skin, rays of gold turn slowly.

0.1 to 1%, candidate two: ice rift

The card freezes: frost crystals grow in from its edges and corners, now and then one melts away and grows again elsewhere, glints flash on the ice, and a cold mist drifts along the foot.

Under 0.1%: plasma, the prize card

Plasma burns inside the card, from its foot up behind the skin. Sparks rise on a swaying path, flicker and fade out slowly. The frame, in the rarity's colour, rises and falls like an equalizer. A crown: large and cut off in the lower right corner, or small on the top edge, kept within the 8px to the header.
