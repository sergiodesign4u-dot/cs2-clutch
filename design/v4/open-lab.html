The open

The moment the case gives up its item, node 3.5. Every row below runs the same engine, so every row keeps the same three rules: the result is fixed before anything moves, the reel never slows past something better than what you got, and it stops inside the card, never on its edge. What differs is how it looks while it runs. In the last second and a half each frame takes the colour of what is actually landing, an honest build up rather than a fake one. Pick the item to land on, or leave it to the odds, and open a row or all of them. Row A is on the case screen now; B and C open there too.
