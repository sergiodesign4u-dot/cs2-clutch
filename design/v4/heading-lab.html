Section headings

Six directions for the headings on Home, each drawn on Home's own titles with the words unchanged, at full width and at 360. They are ideas to pick from; nothing on Home changes until one is chosen. Every one is built from what the site already has: the button stripes, the pink edged pill with its tail, coin 6's bolt, logo 10, the tier and mode colours, and Roboto alone.

Today on Home

Featured cases

Recommended: Slab and its line

It joins the two the founder liked: Mode slab's colour and cut end, now with each section's mark, and Brand bolt's line fading to the edge, drawn in the slab's own colour. The slab says where a section starts and the line says how far it reaches, so neither has to be large. If the slab turns out loud across a whole page, Menu tile keeps the same colours on a small tile and ties every heading to the side menu.

Round 2

Founder of 2 Nov 2026, on round 1: Mode slab "не плохой вариант", to be seen again with an icon and with its slab running to the end of the content and fading; Brand bolt liked, "но надо с иконками обычными а не логотипом"; and more ideas. Each section gets its own mark from the set in _theme.css, the same marks the side menu and the footer use: Featured cases a star, Daily cases the calendar, Before you spend the shield, What opening a case here involves the case, Live drops the diamond. The words are Home's own; Daily's sub line is the one Home prints now.

Round 1

Brand bolt

Logo 10's mark, the pink disc with the gold bolt, replaces the thumbs up, and the hairline becomes a 2px pink line that fades out.

The quietest change from today. It spends the logo on every heading, so the logo is seen a lot more and noticed a little less.

Tab

The title sits on a striped pink tab, the button's own stripes and radius, rooted on a 2px pink line that runs to the edge. On scroll the line draws out of the tab.

The pink striped fill is the button's language, so a heading in it can look pressable. The tab shape and the line under it are what keep it a label.
