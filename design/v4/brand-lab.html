Logo and coin ideas

Round 2: twelve logos built on the lightning bolt, each drawn as a pair with coin 6 and each a full lockup with a worked wordmark. Round 1 and its fourteen logos are kept below, collapsed. Clutch is a working name.

Recommended

Three to look at first, in order.

  1. 12. Bolt counter

    It is the only one where the bolt does the work of a letter and an icon at once: the C of the word, the closed rail mark at 20px and the bolt on coin 6 are the same shape in the same gold, so the pair is built in, not matched afterwards. It reads as CLUTCH at 38px; at 20px the bolt carries it more than the C does.

  2. 3. Charged tick

    It keeps what you liked in Last tick and answers why it looked like an icon: the crosshair is now the C of the word, so it reads as a name. The gold bolt at its centre ties it to coin 6. Its weak point is 20px, where the ticks get thin.

  3. 10. Twin metal

    The closest pair with coin 6: the same rim, face and bolt, struck in pink. Side by side in the header they read as one family. Its risk is the oldest one, a disc next to a word, which is why the bolt breaks the ring and the word starts under it.

Before you pick

  • One bolt. Every logo starts from coin 6's own bolt drawing, the same six points, scaled, cracked, faceted or drawn as a line. None was traced or taken from a real brand's bolt, and no visual similarity search was run.
  • Gold. The logos use coin 6's gold, #FFC44D with #FFE9A8 at the top, so the logo and the coin match. The site token --color-gold is #FADA62, paler and more yellow. They differ today; one of them has to move, and that is a decision, not a side effect of this page.
  • Fonts. Every wordmark is outlined into paths, so no SVG loads a font. The fonts are Kanit, Anton, Rajdhani, Saira Condensed, Chakra Petch, Black Ops One, Russo One, Big Shoulders Display, Exo 2, Sora and Oxanium, each listed under the OFL in the Google Fonts repository (github.com/google/fonts, folder ofl, checked 2 November 2026). Logo 7 uses no font.
  • Not checked. No trademark search was done for the name or for any of these marks. A bolt in a circle (7, 10) or a shield (6) is a crowded form and the most likely to sit close to something already registered.
