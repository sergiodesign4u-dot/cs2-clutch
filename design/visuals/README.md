# The visuals

The product's own images, one set in one colourway, with the prompt that made each written down so that the next image comes out the same. Stage 07 step 4, `D-233`. The kit cites each file through its `--art-*` variable in `design/kit/kit.css`, so a screen never names a file; the kit page, `design/kit/kit.html`, shows the whole set side by side in its Inventory section, block Visuals.

The set is chosen by the photo column of `design/kit/inventory.md`, not by taste: every component that column marks has its image here, and none is a stock photo. 50 files: 46 generated by Codex on `gpt-5.6-terra` at medium effort between 21 October and 22 November 2026, three drawn in canvas code, one composed from two Codex images. The first 42 moved here from `design/v4/art/` and `design/concept/assets/home/` by `git mv` on 19 November 2026, every citing path following and no picture changed.

**Not in this folder, because they are not ours to draw:** the skins (Steam's own item pictures, `design/concept/assets/skins/`, never generated), the providers' marks on sign in and payment (their own logos, masks in `kit.css`), the logo and the coin (the brand lab, `D-184`, `design/v4/art/brand/`), the language flags (inline SVG in `kit.css`).

## The colourway

**Everything that is light, ground or a person is the brand's night:** the navy ground `#060A1E` (`--color-ground`) lit in pink `#FB3F8A` (`--color-pink`) and blue `#65A3FF` (`--color-blue`). **A thing carries its own colour only on itself:** a case its theme, a Daily tier its metal, a mode its card's colour (`--gradient-mode-*`: Cases pink, Battles blue against red, Showdowns red, Upgrade gold).

Measured on 19 November 2026 in a browser on the first 42 files, and by the same measure on 22 November 2026 on the eight covers of `D-246`: of the pixels that carry colour (saturation at least .35, value at least .25), the share of each hue family. The 20 images of the light layer (banner, brand art, rare marks, avatars) are pink and blue, at least 93% of each one's coloured pixels; the rest is the ice cyan of the event title (5%) and red sparks on the agents (3% at most). The 22 of the content layer carry the colour of what they show. The base case, charcoal by `D-230`, and the Silver tier have no colour on purpose. The column Colour in the table below is that measure.

## How a new image is made

1. **Generator.** Codex through the plugin, `--model gpt-5.6-terra --effort medium`, never the default astra. Its image tool takes no size argument, so the size is written in the prompt. Founder decision of 20 October 2026, `D-222` point 5.
2. **Prompt.** The shared block below first, then the prompt of the nearest kind from the list at the end, changed only where the new image differs. A new case cover takes `cases/base.webp` as its input image; a new Daily tier takes `daily/silver.webp`.
3. **Key.** A cut-out on `#00FF00` is keyed to transparency (`ffmpeg` `chromakey=0x00FF00:0.16:0.06`, then despill); a light layer on black turns its brightness into alpha.
4. **Size.** `cwebp` to the size its component draws: a case or a tier 900x600, mode art 640x640, an avatar 256x256, a banner layer 2304x1024.
5. **Register.** The file goes into its component's folder here, a row into the table below with its prompt, an `--art-*` variable into `kit.css` with its origin, and the kit page is regenerated so that its contact sheet shows the file.

Skins are never generated, and the baseline's own branded art is never published.

### The shared block

Written from the phrases that recur across the 45 prompts behind the files: `#00FF00` in 27, "no text" in 25, "preserve" in 27, "realistic" or "believable" in 19, "matte" and "rim light" in 12 and 11, `#FB3F8A` in 10.

```
Original game key art for Clutch, a CS2 case-opening site. Premium PBR game render, believable matte physical materials, restrained lighting, crisp readable silhouette.
Palette: the brand's night. Ground #060A1E, light in hot pink #FB3F8A and electric blue #65A3FF. A thing carries its own colour only on itself: a case its theme, a Daily tier its metal, a mode its card colour.
Background: a cut-out object stands on uniform flat pure chroma green #00FF00, no shadow, glow, gradient or green spill on the object; a light layer (glow, burst, aurora) stands on uniform pure black #000000 for screen blend.
Edits: when an input image is given it is the edit target, not a style hint; preserve its camera, silhouette, proportions and framing, change only what is named.
Constraints: no text unless an exact stencil word is asked for; no logos, watermark, UI; no real people or known game characters; no weapon skins.
```
