Three directions, one brand

The palette, Roboto, the radius ladder and the Solar icons are the same in all three, read from DESIGN-artifacts.md. What a direction changes is the visual layer: density, rhythm, the shape of the item card, how rarity and the price are marked. Each one also settles the baseline's contradictions its own way, baseline.md 13.5: thirteen type sizes, four button heights, and a white label on the pink that fails AA.

Updated after publication, 20 October 2026. The founder picked 2, The stage, D-187, and asked why the screen carried blocks the baseline does not have. The first version drew all three directions on a case layout of its own: the art beside the text, a meta line no node holds, and no Best drops, Skin prices or Before opening. That is neither the baseline's case screen nor node 3.3, and the composition is inherited, CLAUDE.md, Baseline. The stage is now drawn on the 3.3 skeleton, every block in its order with its words from microcopy.md, and only the visual layer is the direction's. Directions 1 and 3 stay below on the old layout as the record of what was not chosen.

Skins are the real items, images from the Steam CDN. Case art is generated in our own style, D-183. Which rarity step each tier takes is an assumption, [?] in DESIGN-artifacts.md.

How this case works

The price buys one round, and the round lands on one item. Every item's chance is printed as a percentage and as a ticket range: the range is what the round resolves against, which is what makes a result checkable.

How a round is checked

The round is fixed before the animation starts and its hash is on screen when you open. After the reveal, one link checks the round against that hash.
