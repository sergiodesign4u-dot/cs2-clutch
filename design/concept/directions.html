Three directions, one brand

The palette, Roboto, the radius ladder and the Solar icons are the same in all three, read from DESIGN-artifacts.md. What changes is composition: density, rhythm, the shape of the item card, where rarity and the price sit. Each one also settles the baseline's contradictions its own way, baseline.md 13.5: thirteen type sizes, four button heights, and a white label on the pink that fails AA.

Skins are the real items, images from the Steam CDN. Case art is generated in our own style, D-183. Every figure, the case name and its counts are samples, D-124. Which rarity step each Steam grade takes is an assumption, [?] in DESIGN-artifacts.md. Pick one by number and name.
