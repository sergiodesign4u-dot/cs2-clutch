# Stage 06. Concept

**What this file is.** The two inputs every visual decision of stage 06 must name as its parent: the founder's taste, written down, and the attribute pairs built from research data, the taste and a technique from `references.md`. A colour, a shape or a type value with no pair here is invented. A reference is never a third parent: `references.md` says how something is done, a pair here says why it fits this product.

**Visible place.** The sections "Designer's taste" and "Attributes" on `design/concept/concept.html`, built at step 5, the attributes as a table with their source rows. Until step 5 this file has no page, the same as `references.md`.

**Route.** Brand exists, no system: `CLAUDE.md`, "Locked decisions", amended by `D-18`, `D-166` and `D-181`. The look is daddyskins.com's own, collected in `research/docs/baseline.md` section 13 and made consistent here.

## 1. Designer's taste

Written on 19 October 2026 from the founder's own words, not inferred:

> "на деддискинс не нравится что все такое устаревшее, острые углы, какое то оно такое плоское, статичное, скучное, вот нравится skin.club и skin.fans и оно такое веселое, игровое, активное, привлекающее внимание ... чтоби не було скучним, статичним, ai slop какойнить точно. Оно должно бути таким, енергичнім, привлекающим внимание, чтоби хотелось зайти и вернуться обратно на сайт"

**Liked, named products.**
- **skin.fans**: fun, game-like, active, attention-grabbing. Primary reference for finish, `references.md` 3.1.
- **skin.club**: the same words. Two details, `references.md` 3.2.

**Anti-references, named.**
- **daddyskins.com as it is today**: dated, sharp corners, flat, static, boring. The product being redesigned is its own first anti-reference, at the level of finish only: its structure, colour, voice and type stay, `D-181`.
- **AI slop.** The founder named it without listing it. Translated below into markers that can be checked on a screen, **awaiting the founder's confirmation**, because a translation is not the founder's words:
  1. Glass cards, blurred translucent panels.
  2. Gradient blobs and glows with no job: every glow lights one thing, the primary action or a rarity, `references.md` S3 and C1.
  3. Icons sitting in circles as decoration.
  4. Radii and shadows chosen by eye rather than taken from one ladder, `references.md` S1.
  5. A hero of generic stock or generated art instead of the product's own skins and case art.

  One note the founder should see: the inherited register, blue-violet grounds with a pink primary, `baseline.md` section 5, sits close to the model's own universal default, the blue-violet gradient. Its origin is `наявний бренд`, so it stays. The guard against slop therefore cannot be "no violet"; it is the five markers above.

**What "come back" may and may not mean.** The founder's wish that people want to return is met by the look and the reveal, not by return mechanics: no streak, status or session score enters through the visual, `CLAUDE.md`, "Product substance", the compliance paragraph.

## 2. What the founder's words mean in pixels

The founder's adjectives are the same words the research recorded about people, so they are read as one signal from two sources, not as taste against data.

| Founder's word | The same thing in research | Where the baseline shows it, measured | What the references do instead |
|---|---|---|---|
| sharp corners, dated | none in research; founder only, `D-182` point 4 | 1 830 square surfaces against ten radius values, `baseline.md` 13.2, 13.5 point 1 | one radius family, `references.md` S1 |
| flat | `personas.md`, The Opener, "Pains": reveals that feel "visually flat"; `research.md`, "5 Key Conclusions", point 5: "DaddySkins' current implementation is flat" | two shadows in four pages, `baseline.md` 13.5 point 8 | low layered shadow, S2; a lit primary, S3 |
| static, boring | `personas.md`, The Opener, "Trust triggers": scared off by a platform that "feels slow or visually dead"; convinced by one that "feels alive" | thirteen type sizes with the top of the scale, 30px, spent on the footer statistics strip, `baseline.md` 13.1: no step of scale says where to look first | one lit action per screen, S3; rarity that glows, C1 |
| fun, game-like | `jtbd.md`, primary job: "a chance at a rare skin I actually want, with a reveal that feels real" | rarity is a gradient behind the item only, `baseline.md` 13.3 | rarity in two marks that agree, S4 and C1 |
| attention-grabbing, energetic | `CLAUDE.md`, principle 4: "dark and electric, never heavy" | the one accent, `#E94D8B`, fills or edges every button, filled and ghost alike, `baseline.md` 13.3 | one primary lit, the rest quiet, S3 |

**Motion is not here.** "Static" is answered on this stage by light, depth and scale, which read as alive on a still screen; motion itself is stage 11, `D-181`.

### 3.1 Where the data and the taste pull apart

**"Game-like" has lost two of its carriers.** On skin.fans most of the playfulness is carried by its cartoon illustration and its condensed display face, DIN Pro Cond, seen on `ref_skinfans_1440_19oct.png`. Both were rejected: the illustration as skin.fans's own voice, `references.md` 3.1, the display face by `D-182` point 5, Roboto only. So A4 puts the game feel on what this product already owns: the skins, their rarity colour, the baseline's own case and banner art, `walk7_home_1440_19oct.png`, and a step of scale in Roboto 900. If that is not enough, the founder decides between two ways back, and neither is taken without that decision:
- Roboto Condensed for headings only, a sibling of the inherited family, which would amend `D-182` point 5;
- an illustration layer of our own, which no stage has scoped.
