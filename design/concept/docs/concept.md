# Stage 06. Concept

**What this file is.** The two inputs every visual decision of stage 06 must name as its parent: the founder's taste, written down, and the attribute pairs built from research data, the taste and a technique from `references.md`. A colour, a shape or a type value with no pair here is invented. A reference is never a third parent: `references.md` says how something is done, a pair here says why it fits this product.

**Visible place.** `design/concept/concept.html`, built at step 5: the taste and the attributes in "Attributes", the logo and the plate in "Brand and references", the imagery in "Imagery", the layout choice in the intro.

**Route.** An inherited visual language, no system: `CLAUDE.md`, "Locked decisions", amended by `D-18`, `D-166` and `D-181`. The look is the baseline's, collected in `research/docs/baseline.md` section 13 and made consistent here.

## 1. Designer's taste

Written on 19 October 2026 from the founder's own words, not inferred. The block quotes keep them as written, with the spelling normalised in this first one; the tables quote them in normalised spelling.

> "на деддискинс не нравится что все такое устаревшее, острые углы, какое то оно такое плоское, статичное, скучное, вот нравится skin.club и skin.fans и оно такое веселое, игровое, активное, привлекающее внимание ... чтоби не було скучним, статичним, ai slop какойнить точно. Оно должно бути таким, енергичнім, привлекающим внимание, чтоби хотелось зайти и вернуться обратно на сайт"

**Liked, named products.**
- **skin.fans**: fun, game-like, active, attention-grabbing. Primary reference for finish, `references.md` 3.1.
- **skin.club**: the same words. Two details, `references.md` 3.2.

**Anti-references, named.**
- **The baseline as it is today**: dated, sharp corners, flat, static, boring. The baseline is the first anti-reference, at the level of finish only: its structure, colour, voice and type stay, `D-181`.
- **AI slop.** The founder named it without listing it. It was translated into checkable markers and the founder confirmed them with amendments, in their words: "да, но с нюансами, прям все отказаться тоже не хочеться вот те же рефернси там у них есть свечения, градиенти, иконки в кружочках может бути, но в целом да иконок должно убти багато и еще и интерактивніх, там всякие штуки например бордери цветние сбоку со скруглениями и так далее, по генерациям скорее всего ми будем генерировать картинки под баннера, может по кейси, но тоже какие наши уникальние стильние кейси а вот скині". So slop is not a list of banned elements but one test: **an element that carries no job and no rarity.**
  1. **Glass cards, blurred translucent panels.** Out.
  2. **A glow or gradient with no job.** Out. Glows and gradients as such stay, as the references use them: each lights one thing, the primary action or a rarity, `references.md` S3 and C1.
  3. **Icons as filler.** Out. Icons themselves are wanted, many of them and interactive, and an icon may sit in a circle where the circle is the control.
  4. **Radii and shadows chosen by eye** rather than taken from one ladder, `references.md` S1. Out.
  5. **Art that is not ours.** Out: generic stock, and generated art in no style of our own. In: generated banners, and generated case art in a style of our own. **Skins are never generated**: a skin is the real in-game item, because the job is "a rare skin I actually want", `jtbd.md`.

  One note the founder saw: the inherited register, blue-violet grounds with a pink primary, `baseline.md` section 5, sits close to the model's own universal default, the blue-violet gradient. Its origin is `наявний бренд`, so it stays. The guard against slop therefore cannot be "no violet"; it is the test above.

**Liked details, named by the founder.**
- **Many icons, interactive.** The icon set is a zone of the step 3 plate; how an icon answers a hover or a press is stage 11.
- **Coloured side borders, rounded.** A coloured edge down one side of a rounded card, as on the references. **Withdrawn by the founder on 20 October 2026, `D-189`**, on seeing it drawn: "мне не нравится бордер слева". The rarity is carried as light instead, `D-188`, and by a 2px line along the foot of the card, `D-190`, picked by the founder: "давай сделаем линию".

**Where this taste meets impeccable's floor.** impeccable, the skill stage 06 runs under from step 3, refuses side stripes and flags glows in its craft floor, `impeccable/reference/degraded/finish-reviewer.md`, step 6 "Floor". The founder's taste wins here, because taste is data and impeccable says how, not what. So both are allowed **only with a job**: the side border carries the item's rarity colour, the glow lights the primary action or a rarity. Step 8's audit reads such a stripe or glow as a decision with this paragraph as its parent, and anything else as a defect.

**What "come back" may and may not mean.** The founder's wish that people want to return is met by the look and the reveal, not by return mechanics: no streak, status or session score enters through the visual, `CLAUDE.md`, "Product substance", the compliance paragraph.

Both were rejected: the illustration as skin.fans's own voice, `references.md` 3.1, the display face by `D-182` point 5, Roboto only. The two ways back, both rejected:

## 4. Logo

**Decided by `D-184` and `D-185` on 19 October 2026.** The G2-b symbol beside the A1-a wordmark, both from `design/concept/assets/logo-styles-r3.png`, traced from the picture. The kit sheet is `design/concept/assets/logo-kit.png`; the masters are in `design/concept/assets/logo/`, rebuilt by `trace.sh`.

**Parent.** A1, alive, the tilt, the glow and the streaks; A2, rounded and of one piece, the rounded letters; A4, game-like, the founder's own words for skin.club and skin.fans. The gold dot is the coin colour, `baseline.md` section 5.

**Versions.** Horizontal, the default, in the rail; stacked, for square and tall places; the symbol alone, in the closed rail and as the app icon; the wordmark alone only where the symbol stands nearby; a flat master for print and wherever glow cannot render; white and black one-colour versions; a small-size cut for 16 to 23px.

**Clear space** is the diameter of the gold dot on every side. **Minimum size** on screen: the horizontal lockup 40px high, the smallest at which CS2 still reads; the symbol 24px; below that the small-size cut. Print sizes and CMYK or Pantone values are `[?]`: print is not in round 1.

**Colour.** Pink `#FB3F8A` (`піксель плейта`, the brand primary since `D-185`), blue `#65A3FF` and gold `#FADA62` (`наявний бренд`), white. The screen version adds tints mixed from these and sphere shades sampled from the picture; they draw light and volume and are not brand values.

**Backgrounds.** The screen version on the product's dark grounds, `#060A1E`, `#0F0E2B` and `#090827`; white on art, banners and photos; black on light grounds; never the screen version on a light ground, where its white letters vanish.

**Never.** Stretch it, recolour it, straighten or re-tilt the word, rearrange the parts, add effects of our own, set it on a busy ground, bring back the spark, or put it in a pill or chip, where it reads as a button, Material as quoted in `D-21`. It lives in the rail, never in the header, `CLAUDE.md`, "Baseline".

## 7. Layout

**Chosen on 20 October 2026: direction 2, The stage, `D-187`**, from the three on `design/concept/directions.html`. The case is the hero on its stage, one pink act, larger cards with a rounded side border in the rarity colour, removed by `D-189`.

**A direction owns the visual layer only.** The first drawing put all three on a case layout of its own; the founder asked why the screen carried blocks the baseline does not have, and The stage was redrawn on node 3.3 block for block, with its words from `voice/docs/microcopy.md`. Composition is inherited, `CLAUDE.md`, "Baseline".

**What it fixes.** Type scale 13, 15, 18, 24, 40; one button height, 56px; a white 20px weight 900 label on the pink, large text at 3.40:1; radius M on cards and L on the stage; the 4px rarity edge with its glow, removed by `D-189`. Each value with its origin is in `DESIGN-artifacts.md`.

**Changed after the stand, `D-188`.** The founder picked three of five weak points named back to them. The stage is lit: beams, a pad, a floor line, the case floating over its reflection. Drop cards carry rarity as light as well as the side border, a pad under the skin and a tint from the foot, and fall from about 400px to 280px with the chance as a tag on the art. The case figures are drawn: 40px numbers beside gauges and seals, and each tier's published share as a bar with the observed tick.

**On the etalon, step 6, `D-191`.** The language moved onto Home, `design/index.html` and `design/index-account.html`, without a new decision about what it is. Where it signs: A3, one lit action per state, Sign in for a guest and Open a case once an account exists, the deposit a quiet pink ring; A4, the feed tile's rarity line and the skins themselves, and the five daily tiers in five rarity steps; A1, the banner lit as the stage is lit, with the case floating on it; A5, the four figures before spending set larger than the footer's counters, after the critique said the counters were the loudest numbers on the page. One light per screen: a blue glow sits on the banner and on the live mode, and nowhere else at rest. No attribute had to change.

**Changed on the Home, `D-192`.** The founder looked at the first coloured Home and asked for the baseline's look first: "надо переделивать все, так чтоби єто було похоже прямо на деддискинс хотя би а потом уже отталкиваться что можно улучшить". The copies now carry the baseline's visual layer as it is, with our logo, words, art and pink; the stage, the rarity light and line, the lit action and the figures above the counters wait on the stand, to return one at a time. The attributes do not change: what changed is which of them the Home answers now, A1 and A4 through the art, A5 through the numbers, and A2 and A3 later.

**Second pass on the Home, `D-193`.** The founder found it still far from the baseline's finish and named the icons. Icons are filled now, the ticker drops stand on hexagons, risk reads as three skulls, and eight new Codex images bring the mode cards and the case covers to the baseline's level, every case a different object. A1 answers through the covers, each case its own stage; A4 through the skin laid on each cover. No attribute had to change.

**Third pass on the Home, `D-194`.** The founder asked for every element of the baseline in detail. The ticker, the mode card, the case card, the icons and the width now follow the baseline's computed styles element by element; the covers and mode arts are Codex variations of the baseline's with our emblems and real skins; the banner moves in four layers. A2, motion that serves emotion, answers on the Home for the first time, through the banner. No attribute had to change.

**Fourth pass on the Home, `D-195`.** The banner title is drawn in ice as the baseline draws its own, the two text blocks before the footer sit on plates, the Daily tiers run two to a row on a phone, and the ground carries faint light. A2 answers again through the title, which now belongs to the moving banner rather than lying on it. No attribute had to change.

**Fifth pass on the Home, `D-196`.** On the founder's marks: the ground goes back to the baseline's flat dark with one picture at the top, the case covers are redrawn gritty and themed, the mode cards, the Daily panel and the footer take the baseline's measures, and the rail's sound and language become one row of two cells. A4, dark and electric and never heavy, answers through the ground: the light the earlier passes added read as a lighter page, not as energy. No attribute had to change.

**Sixth pass on the Home, `D-197`.** On the founder's second round of marks: the cards take the radius the scale already names for cards, `--radius-m`, where the baseline keeps a square corner, so shape language parts from the baseline here by the founder's word, `D-181` allowing it; the four mode arts are redrawn as one family with the case covers, worn and matte under one pink and blue light; the rail shows the eight later ways to play as rows marked Coming soon, as a trial. A4 holds: the rounding softens the frame and not the energy, which stays in the art and the light. No attribute had to change.

**Rejected.** 1, The shelf, the least lift in finish; 3, The ledger, the calmest, leaving the reveal to carry the energy alone. Both stay on the directions page as shown.
