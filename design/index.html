What opening a case here involves

A case is a fixed set of CS2 skins with a published chance on each one. You pay its price in coins, one round decides the item, and you can keep it, sell it or send it to your Steam inventory. Every case shows its chances, its item values and its tested return before you open it, and every round can be checked afterwards.

About Clutch

Clutch is a CS2 case opening site built so that its own numbers can be checked. Every case shows the chance of each skin, its value and its tested return before you spend, and every round leaves a proof you can recompute.

CS2 cases with published odds

Each case is a fixed set of CS2 skins. Its page lists every item with its chance and its current value, so you see what a case pays before you open it, not after.

Provably fair rounds

Every round leaves a proof. You can recompute it yourself, without an account, on the Provably fair page, and a round that does not match is something you can show us.

Skins you can send to Steam

An item from a case waits in My items until you choose: keep it, sell it for coins or send it to your Steam inventory. You sign in on Steam's own page, and we never ask for your password.

Play with limits

Clutch is for people 18 or over. Deposit limits, session limits, a cool down and self exclusion are on the Responsible play page, and no limit ever stops you sending items to Steam.
