## Principles

### Principle 1. Straight answers from someone on the scene

**Rule:** answer the way the baseline's FAQ does: the short answer first, then the fact, in "we" and "you", with nothing padded around it.

**Example:** "No. We never message you first." · "Sold. 12.90 coins are on your balance." · "Yes: it stays in My items until you send it."

**Anti-example:** "We kindly inform you that your request is being processed by our dedicated team." · "Thank you for your patience while we look into this for you."

**Source:** `baseline.md` section 11.2, the answers register: "Yes, we do.", "We do not recommend you do that." The reader is The Opener, an 18 to 25 CS2 player inside skin culture, `personas.md` "Persona 1", Context. The category arrives already suspected, "Random people recommending a site should be a trigger.", `cjm-evidence.md` U-10, and padding reads as the thing being hidden.

### Principle 2. The number, never the superlative

**Rule:** where the category says best, trusted or fair, we print the figure or the check that would prove it, and nothing in its place.

**Example:** "RTP 94.2 %, tested. Expected value 11.68 coins at this cost." · "Median time to Steam 1 h 40 m. Nine in ten arrive within 6 h 15 m." · "Check this round"

**Anti-example:** "The most trusted CS2 case opening site" (the baseline's own meta description) · "a provably fair system that ensures transparency" · "fast withdrawals"

**Source:** `research.md` section 11: all six sites in the category use a superlative with no number behind it and call themselves provably fair as a badge word. The people reject exactly that: "A site that uses 'Provably Fair' as its main advertising slogan, but refuses to actually prove its fairness", `cjm-evidence.md` U-11b row 2. `CLAUDE.md` principle 1 and `D-14`: the product makes its own numbers checkable, and that is the claim, not a verifier as persuasion.

### Principle 3. Money lines are flat: how much, which way, waiting on whom

**Rule:** every line where money or an item moves names the amount with its unit, the direction it moves, and who the wait is on, in the baseline's money sentence and with no excitement in it.

**Example:** "Based on the market price, 2.50 coins goes onto your balance, making it 76.70." · "Waiting on Steam. Steam sets this." · "Nothing was charged."

**Anti-example:** "Your withdrawal is on its way!" · "Instant withdrawals!" · "Processing..."

**Source:** `baseline.md` section 11.2: the money register carries no exclamation mark at all, the only register besides explaining that does not. The floor of the map is here: "its been 30hours for a withdraw. support doesnt answer.", daddyskins, `cjm-evidence.md` U-09 row 1; "The ticket is closed, still $0 added to balance.", U-04 row 1. `CLAUDE.md` principle 3: odds, cost and value visible where money is about to be spent.

### Principle 4. The open gets the energy; the outcome stays open

**Rule:** the act may be short and quick, "Open", "Open again"; no line promises a win, calls a case lucky, or frames an open as profit, before or after it.

**Example:** "Open for 12.40 coins" · "Nine items, one open." · "Opening. The result was fixed before the animation started."

**Anti-example:** "Open your lucky case!" · "multiply your prize with every win!" · "an audience willing to have fun and profit out of it" (all three the baseline's)

**Source:** five of six sites promise winning, `research.md` section 11, and that is where the voice parts from the category. `CLAUDE.md` compliance: "a paid chance, never an investment". The primary job keeps "genuine unpredictability" and a reveal "that feels real", `jtbd.md`, so a promise ahead of the reveal spends the very thing the open is for. The people name the gap between the promise and the item: "i thought i was going to get a covert but actually i got complete dog", `cjm-evidence.md` U-01a row 3.

### Principle 5. A stop says what happened and what still works

**Rule:** a refusal, an error or an empty state says in one line what happened, then what still works or what to do next; it is never alarmed and never a dead end.

**Example:** "Nothing went through: the smallest deposit is $5.00." · "Nothing has been sent to Steam and nothing is lost." · "No rolls yet. Open a case and it is listed here."

**Anti-example:** "ATTENTION!!! Before you submit this ticket, please review the ARTICLES HERE" · "History is empty..." · "The page you are looking for is not available!" (all three the baseline's)

**Source:** `baseline.md` section 11.2, the stops register: curt, which is kept, and alarmed or dead-ended, which is not. The people describe being left with no next step: "I made a deposit via crypto but my balance hasn't been credited. I emailed support but received no response.", `cjm-evidence.md` U-04 row 3. `cjm-to-be.md` T8: winning treated as suspicious behaviour, `B8-3`, is a barrier this product removes, so a stop never reads as an accusation.
