Before you ask, three limits

  • OursA market we do not serve. The verdict and the ground in readable words. It lifts with the staged rollout.
  • Steam'sA trade hold. Steam holds a trade for a period it sets, on conditions it sets. How long is not published here: it is Steam's number and it needs a source and a date before we print it
  • Steam'sA ban on the Steam account. The account cannot receive a trade. This one is not ours and we cannot lift it, and the route to fix it is Steam's.
