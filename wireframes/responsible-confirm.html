Responsible play

What these tools do

Four boundaries you set yourself.

Tightening one takes effect immediately. Loosening one takes 24 hours, and the boundary you have now holds until then.

What a boundary closes, and what it never closes

A boundary can close Adding funds. Opening a case. A session, when its length is reached.
No boundary ever closes Taking what you hold out to Steam. Support. Reading the product.

Deposit limit

Caps what you can put in over a period.

Session limit

Ends a session once it has run this long.

Cool down

Closes adding funds and opening cases for a period you choose.

  • ExtendImmediately.
  • ShortenNot possible. It ends by running out.

Self exclusion

Closes adding funds and opening cases for longer. The one thing here you cannot undo.

  • End earlyNot possible, and that is the point of it.
