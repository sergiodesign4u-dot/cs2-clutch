What the figures are dated to

Cost, worth and chance are all as they were at the roll. A case that is repriced does not rewrite what a past roll cost, and an item that moves does not rewrite what a past roll returned.

What a proof here covers

A proof shows the round was fixed before you clicked and not changed after. It does not show that the published chance was the chance used: that is checked on the case screen.
