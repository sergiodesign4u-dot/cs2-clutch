What the numbers on a case mean

Every tile shows the entry cost of one open and the risk band of the case, High, Medium or Low, read from its drop table. Open a case to see every item with its chance, its current value and the ticket range the roll resolves against. The daily case is earned by wagering, and the tier you reach decides which case it opens.
