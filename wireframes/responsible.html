Responsible play

What these tools do

Four boundaries. You set them, they hold, and none of them is a game.

Tightening one takes effect immediately. Loosening one takes 24 hours, and the boundary you have now holds until then.

What a boundary closes, and what it never closes

A boundary can close Adding funds. Opening a case. A session, when its length is reached.
No boundary ever closes Taking what you hold out to Steam. Support. Reading the product.

A limit stops money going in. It never traps what you already hold, and that includes self exclusion.

Spend ceiling

Caps what you can put in over a period. You can also set it at the moment you add funds, and it is the same one ceiling.

  • TightenImmediately.
  • Loosen24 hours later.
Period not set

Session limit

Ends a session once it has run this long.

  • TightenImmediately.
  • Loosen24 hours later.
Period not set

Cool down

Closes adding funds and opening cases for a period you choose.

  • ExtendImmediately.
  • ShortenNot possible. It ends by running out.
Period not set

Self exclusion

Closes adding funds and opening cases for longer. The one thing here you cannot undo.

  • End earlyNot possible, and that is the point of it.
Period not set

None of the four periods above is decided yet. In several markets the shortest self exclusion is set by law rather than by us, so they are not ours to invent.
