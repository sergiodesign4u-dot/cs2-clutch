How a round is fixed before you click

  1. We publish the hash of a server seed before the round is offered. The hash is a commitment: the seed behind it cannot be swapped later without the hash changing.
  2. Your round is settled once, from that server seed, a client seed and a nonce.
  3. The reveal plays back a result that already exists rather than deciding one while you watch.
  4. The server seed is revealed when the seed rotates, and from then on anyone can recompute the round.

Two reasons a round may have no proof to check yet: it predates the published ledger, or its seed has not rotated. Neither means anything went wrong.

The algorithm, published in full

Version 1, published 12 Jan 2026

The computation

HMAC-SHA256 of the server seed, keyed with client seed:nonce. The first eight hex characters, read as a number, modulo 100 000, plus one, is the ticket. The item whose ticket range holds it is the result.

A worked example

Server seed 7c1e…a904, client seed nightjar, nonce 412. HMAC starts 3a9009c1, which is 982 518 209; modulo 100 000 plus one is ticket 18 210, in the range 11 001 to 23 000, USP-S Kill Confirmed. Check this round Its public page

Questions

Can I use my own tool instead of yours?

That is the point. The algorithm is published so that a stranger can write their own and get our answer.

Why can I not check a round from last year?

Rounds from before the published ledger have no commitment behind them, so they cannot be checked here.
