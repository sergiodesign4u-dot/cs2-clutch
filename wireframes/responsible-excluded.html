What these tools do

Four boundaries. You set them, they hold, and none of them is a game.

Tightening one takes effect immediately. Loosening one takes 24 hours, and the boundary you have now holds until then.

What a boundary closes, and what it never closes

A boundary can close Adding funds. Opening a case. A session, when its length is reached.
No boundary ever closes Taking what you hold out to Steam. Support. Reading the product.

A limit stops money going in. It never traps what you already hold, and that includes self exclusion.

Deposit limit
