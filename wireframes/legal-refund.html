In short

How money moves in and out, what happens when a payment fails or is disputed, and what you can get back.

  • One coin is $1.00, and every price here is in coins.
  • The smallest deposit is $5.00. A deposit is usually credited within 2 minutes.
  • A deposit limit you set holds every payment, and raising it takes 24 hours.
  • Items you hold are not covered here: keeping, selling and sending them are in the terms of use.

Where this summary and the document below disagree, the document is the one that governs.
