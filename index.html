Product design pipeline

CS2 Clutch
research to design system

A twelve stage pipeline that turns market research into a working design system. Every stage leaves two outputs: a markdown source of truth for the model, and a page in this browser for the person who decides.

The product is a redesign of a live CS2 skin platform. Structure and information architecture are inherited from the six year old baseline; the visual language is derived from scratch.
